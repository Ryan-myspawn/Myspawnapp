A THIRD GREP FAILURE MODE, AND WITH IT THE WEEK'S SET IS COMPLETE. freeze.health appeared to return 59 files. Searched literally with grep -F, it returns ZERO. The 59 are the regex dot matching the space in "Freeze Health". This lane nearly recorded that we already held the company's domain.

CONSOLIDATED RULE, because three different failures in four days is a pattern rather than three accidents. A grep lies in three directions:

1. UNDER-counting on vocabulary, as with attorney-in-fact against our own "attorney in fact", found 10 November.

2. OVER-counting on our own jargon, as with gate returning "QC Gate", found 11 November.

3. OVER-counting on regex metacharacters, as with freeze.health matching "Freeze Health", found today.

The single habit that catches all three: a count that will be relied on must be re-run in a second form, and for a string containing a dot, a slash or a hyphen, one of those forms is grep -F.
