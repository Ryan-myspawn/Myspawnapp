BONUS DEEP DIVE: CELLSAVE, the rotation's named target.

  • What it is: a cord blood and stem cell bank, a brand of the CSG-BIO Group, operating as several regional entities. US arm CellSave America, Scottsdale, Arizona. Packages cover four tissue sources: cord blood, cord tissue, placenta tissue, amnion placental tissue.
  • Dates are regional, not corporate: CellSave Arabia launched 11 September 2006; CellSave by ADSCC opened its Abu Dhabi lab in 2023. No founding year found for the US entity and none inferred. The "20 years" and "80 countries" figures are company self-description.
  • THE FINDING, and it points at us: CellSave America does not run its own US laboratory. It contracts processing to Cryopoint (BHC LLC), Brownsburg, Indiana and Vitalant New Jersey, Montvale, New Jersey, both FDA-registered and AABB-accredited.
  • Why that is not a criticism: we do the same thing. MySpawn is a brand; GenVault holds the sample. Our claim is not vertical integration, it is disclosure. Any copy implying competitors are deceptive for using a contract lab would be dishonest, because contract processing is the norm and we are part of it.
  • The usable angle, as a question rather than an accusation: "Who physically holds it, and does the company tell you without being asked?" Answerable about us in one sentence; hard work for most of this sector.
  • No US pricing found, not estimated. Fourth competitor in a row whose price we could not establish, after StoryWorth, Acorn and Herasight. In this sector the price sits behind a consultation, and any competitor price in our content without a primary source is a liability.
  • Accreditation precision: CellSave is AABB-accredited and FDA-registered. AABB is not CAP. GenVault's accreditations are GenVault's; neither set is ever used loosely.
