THREADS POSTS

THREADS 1.

Something worth knowing about lab reports, and it is not a conspiracy.

A reference range is built from a reference population. If you are not well represented in that population, the range is a worse guide for you than it looks, and nobody at the lab is doing anything wrong by printing it.

The same is true of every threshold you will meet. Trial entry criteria. Insurance coverage limits. Regulatory cut-offs. Each one exists to make a continuous thing decidable for somebody's specific purpose, and that purpose is usually not you.

I am not telling you to ignore them. They are the best available way to turn a smooth quantity into a yes or a no. I am telling you that a value one point below a line and a value one point above it are nearly the same measurement, and they get two different words attached.

The error is not having a line. The error is thinking the line is a fact about your body.

THREADS 2.

A test for anyone selling you something, including us.

Superior to what. In whom. Measured how.

Those three questions are the whole skill, and they work on any quoted improvement in any field. Most claims survive the first one and fall over on the second.

So here is us, under our own test. Superior to what: we claim superiority to nothing, because we are storage and there is no competing claim to make. In whom: our claims are about samples, not about people, because we do not generate results. Measured how: by a quality system a third party audits and publishes, which you can look up on the accreditor's own register rather than taking it from our website.

MySpawn storage is ninety-nine dollars a year, billed annually. If a sentence of ours ever fails those three questions, that is a sentence we should not be writing.
