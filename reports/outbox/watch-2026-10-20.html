CORRECTION 1, AND IT IS FLAGGED RATHER THAN OVERWRITTEN

This file has recorded since deep-dive #4 on 23 August that $35 per sample per year is an "oncofertility compassionate tier." Today's evidence attributes $35 to the twenty-five-year Forever plan, and Legacy's cancer-treatment page advertises "starts at $35 per sample, per year" while pricing its twenty-five-year bundle at $4,995.

So the "from $35" on the cancer page is the LONG-PLAN RATE PRESENTED AS AN ENTRY PRICE, not a hardship rate.

WE ARE NOT OVERWRITING THE ENTRY, WE ARE DISPUTING IT. It is possible both exist, a compassionate tier and a twenty-five-year plan that happen to land on the same number. Today's sources mention no compassionate tier at all. The baseline entry is flagged DISPUTED pending a direct check, which is how this file handled SecuriGene's $498 for a month before resolving it correctly.

CORRECTION 2: THE $145 FIGURE CANNOT BE RELIED ON, AND THE TRAP FIRED EXACTLY AS PREDICTED

Our entry of 27 August reads "$145 clinical semen analysis, setting new price benchmark." Today the clinician page says $275 standalone and $495 bundled.

Three readings are possible and we are not choosing between them without better evidence: the price rose; $145 was a promotional launch price; or $145 and $275 are different products. "Setting a new price benchmark" is a press release's own language about a launch, which is precisely the trap yesterday's condition named, and it fired on the first figure it was pointed at.

AND A FOURTH POSSIBILITY THAT IS WORSE AND MUST BE RECORDED: a 2024 third-party review lists $145 as Legacy's STORAGE price, not its analysis price. If our baseline attached a storage figure to an analysis product, the error is a category error rather than a staleness one. Flagged. Not asserted.

THE STRUCTURAL FINDING, WHICH OUTLASTS ANY OF THESE NUMBERS

Prepaid term pricing is now observed at THREE companies in this file. Cryo-Cell's eighteen-year prepaid at $5,068 (dive #22). SecuriGene's forensic ladder to $6,850 lifetime (resolved yesterday). And now Legacy's four-tier ladder to $4,995 for twenty-five years.

This file flagged prepaid term storage on 30 August as a third pricing model it did not contain. It now contains three instances of it, and in every case the long plan sells PRICE CERTAINTY rather than savings, because the money is paid up front.

SO WHAT FOR MYSPAWN, and the comparison has to be made honestly or not at all. Their $35 per sample per year requires $4,995 up front. Ours is $99 a year, billed annually, with the first 1,000 members locking the founding rate. The honest contrast is flat-and-low against prepaid-and-large, never one-time against recurring, and it is additionally bounded by the fact that gametes and a dried DNA record are different products with different physics. Threat: low on price, unchanged. Positional value: high, because our founding-rate lock is the same promise shape as their Forever plan at a fraction of the commitment.

STEAL, and it is the same note three dives running because three competitors keep proving it: a prepaid or rate-locked option is what this category's buyers are being offered everywhere else. We already have the shape of it in the founding-rate lock and have never built a page around it.
