QUIET-DAY DEEP DIVE: TOMORROW BIO (the rotation's named target, and it broke a five-deep pattern)

The rotation's instruction was a description and a date. Both obtained. And for the first time in five dives, the PRICE is established too, which is the finding.

WHAT IT IS. A Berlin-based cryonics (the company's term is biostasis) provider selling standby, stabilisation and cryopreservation on a membership model. It is a European operator that entered the US market, which is the only reason it belongs on a US-only brand's watch list at all.

DATE, AND IT IS A TWO-SOURCE SPLIT RATHER THAN A FIGHT: the company's own timeline says founded DECEMBER 2019. Tracxn and CB Insights both list 2020. Recorded as a range with both ends attributed, per the evidence standard, and the company's own date is the earlier one, which is the direction self-reported founding dates usually run.

PEOPLE: Dr. Emil Kendziorra, founder and CEO, with Fernando Azevedo Pinheiro named as co-founder. Consistent across sources.

SCALE, AND IT IS SMALL: 20 people and 10 pets cryopreserved as of 20 January 2025, per the Cryonics Wiki. A 2023 Tech.eu interview said "about ten people" and described those as for TRAINING purposes, which is a DIFFERENT CLAIM and must not be treated as the earlier point on a growth curve. No figure dated after early 2025 was found.

THE STRUCTURAL FINDING, AND IT IS THE THIRD TIME IN THREE DAYS: Tomorrow Bio does not hold its own patients long-term. Long-term care is handled by a PARTNER, the European Biostasis Foundation in Switzerland. CellSave contracts processing to Cryopoint and Vitalant. MySpawn is a brand and GenVault holds the sample. Three consecutive dives, three brands whose storage is somebody else's building. This is the industry's architecture, not a scandal, and any copy implying otherwise about a competitor would be dishonest about us.
