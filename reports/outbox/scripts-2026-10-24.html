SCRIPT 1: "Whoever Goes First"

Length: 48 seconds. Shootable alone. One location, one take, no dialogue partner.

FORMAT NOVELTY, AND IT IS THE FORMAT THAT MAKES THIS SCRIPT WORK RATHER THAN DECORATING IT: the piece is shot forward and PLAYED IN REVERSE. played in reverse returns ZERO fleet-wide. The existing "backwards" hits are a title from September and a piece about walking backwards on 9 October; neither is reverse playback.

Why reverse is not a gimmick here: the subject is that the ORDER of two events decides what a document means. A piece that runs backwards makes the audience feel the thing the rule does. Structurally distinct from all seven preceding script days: no chairs, no black screen with type, no beans, no thermometer, no printed page read aloud, no elevator, no ice, no house move.

Script

[SHOT 1 - plays FIRST, was filmed LAST. Single wide, a kitchen table, two unmarked manila folders lying side by side, presenter's hands flat on the table either side of them. Everything in this piece moves the wrong way: the chair settles, the hands lift. Hold two seconds before the first word.]

VO: "This is a document that means two different things, and nothing about the document changes."

ON SCREEN: WHOEVER GOES FIRST

[SHOT 2 - presenter's hand moves backwards away from the left folder, which it had been resting on]

VO: "Say you set up a trust. Income to your son while he lives, and whatever is left goes to your grandchildren."

[SHOT 3 - same frame, the reverse motion continues, the second folder slides back into place]

VO: "In American tax law, leaving something to a grandchild while their parent is alive is called a skip. There is a tax built specifically to catch it."

[SHOT 4 - hands return to the table edge, reversed]

VO: "There is also an exception. If your son is already dead when it counts, your grandchild moves up a generation and it is not a skip any more."

ON SCREEN CARD, held: THE RULE: IRC 2651(e), BROADENED 1997, TREASURY REGS 2005

[SHOT 5 - the camera is still; only the reversed settling of the room moves]

VO: "Here is the part that gets people. The test is whether he is already dead at the moment the transfer first becomes subject to tax. The earliest such moment. Not the moment your grandchild actually gets anything."

[SHOT 6 - hold, nothing moves]

VO: "So if you fund that trust and your son dies afterwards, the exception does not reach back for you. Same trust. Same family. Different answer, decided entirely by the order in which two things happened."

ON SCREEN CARD: TIMING RULE, NOT AN AMOUNT RULE

[SHOT 7 - the reverse playback ends on the frame that was filmed first: hands not yet on the table, folders untouched. The image resolves into stillness rather than motion, which is only possible in reverse]

VO: "And before anyone panics: the skip tax only starts above about fifteen million dollars a person. It is almost certainly not about you."

ON SCREEN: ABOUT $15M PER PERSON, 2026 / NOT LEGAL OR TAX ADVICE

[SHOT 8 - cut hard on the last syllable, no fade]

VO: "The order still is. Every document you sign assumes a sequence nobody gets to choose."

Sound: no music. Reversed room tone for the whole piece, which sounds subtly wrong without the audience being able to say why, and that is the entire sound design. The voiceover is recorded FORWARD and laid over the reversed picture, so the words are clear while the world is not. Cut audio dead on the final syllable.

BINDING ON THE SHOOT AND THE EDIT, written here so nothing is improvised past it.

  • The $15 million line and its card are NOT optional and cannot be moved earlier. The radar's first binding flag on this direction was that any hook implying the viewer owes this tax is false. The script earns its hook on the timing rule and then disarms the tax in shot 7. Remove that shot and the piece becomes a lie by omission.
  • The "not legal or tax advice" card is not optional.
  • No product card, no price, no URL, no logo end-plate. The radar's third binding flag said a product card must not be bolted to this material. The brand appears nowhere in this script. The static ad below is where the product goes.
  • Never imply MySpawn stores, drafts, administers or advises on any legal instrument. We store a biological sample. If the presenter extemporises a sentence connecting the two, the take is unusable.
  • The reverse must be a true reverse of a single continuous take, not forward footage with reversed clips. If the room does not read as subtly wrong, the format has failed and the piece should be dropped rather than fixed with effects.

[^1]: Predeceased parent rule, Internal Revenue Code section 2651(e), which replaced a narrower provision under the 1997 Act and was broadened to reach taxable terminations and distributions as well as direct skips. The timing test is whether the parent is dead when the transfer is first subject to estate or gift tax, and where there is more than one such time, the earliest. Treasury final regulations: https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-regs/td_9214.pdf

[^2]: The funding-order trap, labelled PRACTITIONER COMMENTARY rather than authority: funding a trust for a child with a remainder to a grandchild does not attract the exception if the child dies after the funding. https://greenleaftrust.com/missives/generation-skipping-transfers-avoiding-a-few-traps-along-the-way/

[^3]: The roughly $15 million per person 2026 exemption, labelled on screen as approximate and sourced here to law-firm commentary rather than an IRS page, consistent with this morning's blog. https://www.millercanfield.com/resources-One-Big-Beautiful-Bill-Estate-Gift-Tax-Exclusion-Generation-Skipping-Transfer-Exemption-Increases-to-15-Million.html
