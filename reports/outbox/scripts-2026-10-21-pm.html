About 52% of inheritances are now received when the recipient is 55 or older. In 2015 that figure was 41%.

Threads 2:

I found out this week that who inherits from you and who decides about you are answered by completely different laws.
Intestacy says who gets your things. A separate statute says who may bury you. A third says who speaks for you if you cannot speak.
In some states those three lists put three different people at the top. In Texas, the person with the right to decide about your remains can lose it if they do not act within six days of being told you died.
Nobody has to behave badly for that to happen. A delayed flight will do it.
If you only do one thing: name a health care agent in writing, and tell them.
