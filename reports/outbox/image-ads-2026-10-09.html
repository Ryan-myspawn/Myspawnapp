NEW CONCEPT 1: Ad_AnExposureIsNotATrend_185

Headline: An exposure is not a trend.

Body: A study reported on 7 October found men with more insecticide in their semen had lower sperm concentration than men with less. 179 men, one fertility clinic, one moment in time. That is a gap between people, not a change over thirty years. Three other recent studies report counts flat or rising. We store a DNA record on purpose, $99 a year, billed annually.

Support: Frontiers in Endocrinology, reported 7 October 2026 via The New Lede; 179 men at an ART clinic in Jinan, China; 27 percent concentration difference between highest and lowest neonicotinoid exposure quartiles. Counter-set: Beijing donors up 12.3 and 18.7 percent 2011 to 2018; an Irish single-centre review up nearly 23 percent; a 2025 US meta-analysis flat. The ad does not state the 27 percent, deliberately: a number without its clinic-population limit does not belong on a static creative where the limit cannot travel with it.

Visual hook: a chalk cliff face in raking light, its horizontal strata legible as bands. The image is a record laid down over decades. The headline is the turn: the study was one day.

Photo: Unsplash TJKPw5RSK_k, snap wander (@passimage), https://unsplash.com/photos/a-rocky-cliff-with-trees-on-top-of-it-TJKPw5RSK_k, 3676x3648, raw https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1701621386671-f34dcde3407d. No people, no faces, no legible brand mark.

FIXED IN THE RENDER. First pass at brightness(0.46) buried the strata, which destroyed the only reason the photo was chosen. Re-rendered at brightness(0.70) contrast(1.14) saturate(0) and re-cropped into the layered face, away from the sky. A darkening setting that hides the subject is not a style, it is a mistake, and it took reading the render to see it.

Placement: paid, all placements.
