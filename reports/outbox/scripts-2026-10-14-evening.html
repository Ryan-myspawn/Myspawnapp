TWO THREADS POSTS

THREADS 1:

There are two ways to keep something safe for fifty years. You keep it, or you have it kept.
Keeping it yourself fails quietly: it ends up in the wrong box during a house move in 2041 and the person who knew what it was has died.
Having it kept fails differently: the institution has to still exist, still be paid, and still care.
Nobody selling you either one should be offering you certainty. We are the second kind, and that is our failure mode, not a feature.

THREADS 2:

A thing we keep learning the expensive way: a description is not the thing.
This week a photo filed as "writing on a wall" turned out to be a political slogan. Another filed as "a ladder" turned out to be two doors.
Both descriptions were written by us, carefully, at the time.
If you keep an archive of anything, the label is a hypothesis until somebody opens the box.
