2 THREADS POSTS

Threads 1

A dead man's switch does something if you stop confirming you are alive.
Almost nobody who builds one gets the trigger wrong. They get what happens next wrong.
The switch fires perfectly and delivers a password you rotated six months ago. It fires and the person you named deleted their account, which silently removed them as your contact. It fires and hands over a credential, which is not the same thing as ownership.
If the payload is credentials, do not ship a copy. Ship access to the live vault.
And test it once a year by actually letting it fire. A switch that has never run to completion is an assumption, not a system.

Threads 2

In 1977 a committee had twelve inches of gold-plated copper and one shot at explaining humanity.
They chose 115 images, nature sounds, greetings in 55 languages, and music. No letter.
Then they did the thing I cannot stop thinking about: they put a speck of uranium on the cover, so whoever finds it can measure what is left and work out how long it has been drifting.
They could not assume a shared calendar. So they shipped the clock instead of the date.
Most of us assume our kids will know what the box in the attic is for. Context is the first thing to go.

Both Threads posts are zero-product by design.
