Threads 2:

"Can a spouse override a will" has a yes attached to conditions, and the conditions are where it lives.

Roughly forty US states give a surviving spouse a statutory share they can claim instead of taking what the will says. At least one state is reported to have no such statute at all. Community property states handle it differently, because a spouse already owns a share during the marriage.

Notice the shape of that. The general rule and its exceptions tend to appear on different pages, and the exceptions are usually the newer information.

Which is a reasonable rule for reading almost anything.
