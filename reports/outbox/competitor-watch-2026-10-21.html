FINDING 1: A PRICE-KIND CORRECTION TO OUR OWN BASELINE

What today's check actually found:

  • $12,500 is Orchid's OWN patient page figure, framed as the cost of standard Preimplantation Whole-Genome Sequencing for an IVF cycle with 5 biopsied embryos. This is a LIST price.
  • $2,500 per embryo comes from LAUNCH-ERA PRESS COVERAGE, described as an additional per-embryo fee on top of IVF. It is derived from coverage, not from the company's current page, and the coverage is from the January 2024 launch window.

AND THE LIST PRICE CARRIES STATED VARIABILITY THAT OUR ENTRY OMITTED. Orchid's own page says costs may vary depending on the clinic and the complexity of a case, that additional testing may incur additional costs, and that options for more limited screening may be available for cost-sensitive patients. A price with three stated escape hatches is a starting point, not a price.
