Format returning ZERO fleet-wide: elevator, lift doors, floor number. Structurally distinct from every script of the last seven days: there is no desk, no paper, no top-down shot, no object being demonstrated, no second pair of hands, and no locked tabletop camera. The camera is inside a moving enclosed space and the only thing that changes is where it is.

Length: 62 seconds. Format: ONE unbroken take inside a lift. Camera at chest height, fixed, facing the doors. He is in frame from behind, shoulders only, never turning. No cuts.

Sound: room tone and the mechanical noise of the car. One chime at 0:44. No music at any point.

[SHOT: doors open, a floor visible beyond. He steps in. Doors begin to close. 0:00]

VO: "You press the button once."

[0:06, doors closed, the car begins to move. Floor indicator visible top of frame.]

VO: "After that you do nothing. You stand in a metal box and you are carried, and the carrying is not something you are doing. It is something that was arranged."

[0:16, indicator changing]

VO: "Somebody built this in nineteen sixty-something. Somebody inspects it on a schedule you have never seen. Somebody renews a contract you do not know the name of."

ON-SCREEN TEXT: one decision · many custodians

[0:28]

VO: "And none of that is visible from in here. From in here it is a button and a number."

[0:36]

VO: "Most things that carry you work like this. You decide once, badly or well, and then a chain of people you will never meet either keeps it running or does not."

[0:44, the chime. Doors begin to open on a different floor.]

VO: "The only part that was ever yours was the button."

[0:50, doors fully open, he does not move yet. Hold.]

VO: "So the question is not whether you can control the chain. You cannot. It is whether you pressed anything at all."

[0:58, he steps out. The doors begin to close on an empty car. Cut to black on the closing doors, NOT on a logo.]

ON-SCREEN TEXT: myspawn.me · $99/year, billed annually

Why it earns a place: it is a sixty-second argument that a single early decision beats continuous control, which is the one thing our product actually offers, and it never claims the chain is safe. Product mention: the end card only. No spoken price.

Risk flags, two. (1) Do not shoot in a lift with a visible building name, floor directory or third-party branding; the frame must carry no legible text. (2) The line "nineteen sixty-something" is deliberately vague and must stay vague. Do not let an editor replace it with a specific year nobody checked.

Your death is not a fact that spreads. It is a notification somebody has to make.
Social Security finds out because a funeral home, a relative or a bank tells it. And under a 2013 law, the full federal death file is withheld from the public for three calendar years.

Write the list while you are alive. Not passwords. Institutions.

I tried to find out which US states allow a self-proving affidavit on a will.
