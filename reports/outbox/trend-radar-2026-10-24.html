Why it is fresh: this is not a topic, it is a repeatable segment, and the fleet has never had one built on ABSENCE of evidence. assay certificate and blood boy return ZERO.

The format: take any viral biotech or longevity claim and ask the four questions today's coverage asked of one. Which laboratory. Where are the results. Is there an assay certificate. Is there an independently reviewed paper. Score the claim out of four on screen. Never name a person in the title card; name the claim.

Why it is the stronger proposal: this operation's actual differentiator is that it refuses figures, and it has been refusing them at a rate of three or four per production run for weeks without ever showing an audience the method. A format built on the refusal turns an internal discipline into content. It also solves the thin-hook problem structurally: it needs no in-window news, only a claim, and there is always a claim.

Risk flag, one and it is the whole of it: the scoring must be applied to a claim we are not selling against, at least the first time. A format that debuts by scoring a competitor is an attack ad wearing a method's clothes. First episode should score a claim in our own favour's direction and find it wanting.
