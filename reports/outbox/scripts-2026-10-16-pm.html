Primary audience: Men 25-55

Trend / hook used: Reporting on CDC data, surfaced in this morning's radar: birth rates fell among mothers under 30 between 2024 and 2025 and rose among mothers over 30.

Length: 52 seconds.

Script

[SHOT 1: him, mid, flat, no music. 0:00]

VO: "Birth rates among mothers under thirty went down between twenty twenty-four and twenty twenty-five."

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: Under 30: down.]

[SHOT 2: same frame, he pauses, then restarts the sentence. NO CUT. 0:07]

VO: "Let me say that again, all of it. Birth rates among mothers under thirty went down between twenty twenty-four and twenty twenty-five, and birth rates among mothers over thirty went up."

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: appears under the first line: Over 30: up.]

[SHOT 3: 0:19]

VO: "Those are the same data. The first version is the one that travels."

[SHOT 4: 0:24]

VO: "And the two halves point in different directions, so the honest headline is not that people stopped having children. It is that they moved when."

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: Not whether. When.]

[SHOT 5: tone drops, slower. 0:33]

VO: "I want to be careful here, because I am about to do the thing I am complaining about if I am not. This is reporting on agency data that I have not opened myself. And one year is not a trend."

[ON-SCREEN TEXT: Reported from CDC data. One year.]

[SHOT 6: 0:43]

VO: "So the test is not whether a number is true. It is whether you were handed all of it."

[SHOT 7: final card, no face. 0:48]

[ON-SCREEN TEXT, two lines held: A statistic with two halves gets quoted in halves. / Ask which half you got.]

[CARD: myspawn.me]

SOUND/MUSIC: none until shot 7, then one low sustained note. The restart in shot 2 must read as a deliberate choice on camera, not an edit. If it is cut, the format is gone.

Platform cut notes

  • Reels: add one second before the restart in shot 2. The 35-to-60 viewer needs a beat to notice the sentence is being repeated on purpose.
  • Shorts: cut shot 3 and run shots 1, 2, 4, 5, 7. Out at 42 seconds.
  • 7-second teaser cut: shot 1 in full, then the first four words of shot 2, then black on "all of it". The teaser is the promise of the missing half.

1. On-screen attribution reads "Reported from CDC data", never "CDC says". The agency release was not opened at its own domain.

2. The 39-percent survey figure stays OUT. A three-point gap against millennials is not a generational finding, and putting it on screen would be the exact error this script is about.

3. Never phrase the over-30 rise as reassurance about any individual's fertility. It is a population statistic and the script must not drift into a personal claim.

4. Shot 5 is not optional. It is the script's own limit, delivered before the close, and removing it makes the piece the thing it criticises.
