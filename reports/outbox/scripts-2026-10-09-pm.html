SCRIPT: "How Big Is the Claim?"

Runtime: ~75 seconds. Platform: Reels and Shorts primary, TikTok secondary.

FORMAT: THE DISTANCE IS THE ARGUMENT. One locked-off wide camera, never moved, never cut. He starts at a middle distance and physically steps BACKWARD for each bigger claim, getting smaller in frame, until he is far away and small. Then he walks all the way up to the lens for the last one. No graphics beyond the on-screen claim text. The piece is a single take.

Sound: room tone, footsteps audible. No music. The footsteps are the edit.

NAMING RULE, BINDING: no company is named on air, in text, or in the captions. The subject is the size of a claim, not a competitor. Naming anybody turns a useful idea into a swipe and puts a right-of-publicity problem into a sixty-second video.

#SHOTVO / ON SCREEN
1[Locked wide. He stands mid-frame, normal distance.]VO: "Everybody in my industry is making a claim. They are not the same size."
2[He takes one step back.]VO: "This one is: we can tell you about your health risks from a sample." [ON SCREEN: a claim about you]
3[Another step back. Smaller in frame.]VO: "This one is bigger: we can tell you which of these embryos will be healthier." [ON SCREEN: a claim about a probability]
4[Another step back.]VO: "Bigger again: we can tell you which one will be smarter, or taller, or live longer." [ON SCREEN: a claim about a person who does not exist yet]
5[He is now far back, small, the room enormous around him.]VO: "At this distance you can barely hear me. That is roughly the relationship between the size of that claim and the evidence under it."
6[Still far. He holds.]VO: "And credit where it is due. At least one company in that corner tests its predictions on actual adult siblings instead of only strangers in a database, which is a much harder test than the one most people run." [ON SCREEN: preprint, 2025. Not peer-reviewed.]
7[Beat.]VO: "The professional bodies still say the science is not there yet for choosing embryos this way." [ON SCREEN: summarised from coverage, not from the bodies' own documents]
8[He starts walking forward, talking as he comes.]VO: "Here is ours, and I want you close for it, because it is small enough that you should be able to check it."
9[He arrives at the lens. Face fills the frame.]VO: "We keep a DNA sample. It stays yours. That is the entire claim." [ON SCREEN: a claim about a person who exists]
10[Hold. Then cut to black.][ON SCREEN: myspawn.me: $99 a year, billed annually.]
