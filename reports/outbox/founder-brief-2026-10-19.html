THE ONE DECISION

Pick one lane and switch it off for a week.

The operation's problem is no longer quality or even delivery. It is that nine lanes keep producing at full rate into queues nobody is draining, and every one of them now spends part of its run reporting that it cannot deliver. Twenty-two unshot scripts, eleven unpostable Reddit articles, four unsent newsletters, fifty-two unread inboxes.

The cheapest version of that decision: turn off the Inbox Concierge until Gmail is fixed. It has run 52 times, produced 52 identical rows, and read nothing. It is the only lane whose output is purely a record of its own inability to run.

The highest-value version: pause the Content Factory and Deep Production for one week and film the backlog instead. Writing is seven times ahead of shooting. Nothing this stack produces matters until something gets published somewhere a customer can see it.
