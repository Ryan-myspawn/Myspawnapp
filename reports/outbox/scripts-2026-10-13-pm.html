Script

[SHOT 1: a left-luggage counter, shot from the customer side, shelving of numbered bags behind it. No faces.]

VO: "There are exactly two ways to keep something safe for a long time. You keep it. Or you have it kept."

[SHOT 2: close on a numbered paper tag.]

VO: "Keeping it yourself has one failure mode, and it is not dramatic. Nobody steals it. It just ends up in the wrong box during a house move in 2041, and the person who knew what it was has died."

[ON SCREEN TEXT: Failure mode one: nobody remembers.]

[SHOT 3: the shelving, wider, the bags in rows.]

VO: "Having it kept has a different failure mode. The institution has to still exist. It has to still be paid. It has to still care."

[ON SCREEN TEXT: Failure mode two: the holder stops.]

[SHOT 4: static on the counter.]

VO: "I am not going to tell you one of those is obviously better. They are different bets and anyone selling you certainty on either one is selling you a feeling."

[SHOT 5: cut to black, white text.]

VO: "Here is ours, stated plainly. We have it kept. Samples sit at GenVault in New Jersey, a third-party biorepository with its own accreditations, which are theirs and not ours, and you should check them at the facility rather than take our word for it."

[ON SCREEN TEXT: GenVault's accreditations are GenVault's.]

[SHOT 6: the counter again, empty.]

VO: "Which means our failure mode is the second one, and I would rather say that out loud than have you work it out later. We depend on a company and a building continuing. In exchange you do not depend on a drawer."

[SHOT 7: hold.]

VO: "Ninety-nine dollars a year, billed annually. First thousand members lock the founding rate. Storage only. We do not make anything from it and nobody can."

Sound: room tone only. No music anywhere in this script. The content is an admission and music would make it sound like a pitch.

Platform cut notes

  • Reels: as written.
  • Shorts: as written, with the two failure-mode cards held a beat longer; this is the one that gets paused and read.
  • 7-second teaser cut: SHOT 1's line alone, over the shelving. "You keep it, or you have it kept." Nothing else.
