COLD OPEN (word for word, about 80 seconds)

There is a sentence going around this field, and it is true. Here it is, as published: "Achieving More Than Double the Success Rate of Standard IVM."
That is from a company press release. Not a journal. A press release, and I will say that again later because it matters.
Now. Double the success rate of what?
Most people hear that and fill in the blank with IVF. The normal thing. The thing their clinic does. And that is not what it says. It says standard IVM, which is a different procedure, a smaller and more specialised one, and the comparison is against another product that does that specialised job.
So I went and read the trial. Not the press release. The registry entry, the public one, the one anybody can open. And the registry says plainly what the comparison is: it lists another in vitro maturation system as an active comparator. The question the trial is built to answer is whether this new thing beats that other thing. Not whether it beats IVF.
Nobody hid this. It is in a public database, written down, free. The gap is not between what the company said and what is true. The gap is between what the sentence says and what the sentence sounds like.
And that gap is the most useful thing I can give you today, because it is not about fertility. It is about every number anyone will ever quote at you.

CLIP MOMENT: "The gap is not between what the company said and what is true. The gap is between what the sentence says and what the sentence sounds like."

SEGMENT 1: The sentence, and where it comes from (4 to 5 min)

VERBATIM QUOTE: "Gameto Announces Landmark Pregnancy Rate for iPSC-Derived Fertilo, Achieving More Than Double the Success Rate of Standard IVM"

SOURCE: BioSpace press-release listing, https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/gameto-announces-landmark-pregnancy-rate-for-ipsc-derived-fertilo-achieving-more-than-double-the-success-rate-of-standard-ivm

EPISTEMIC LABEL, SAID ON AIR: this is a COMPANY PRESS RELEASE, not a peer-reviewed publication.

STANCE: not an accusation. A reading lesson. The headline is accurate on its own terms. Our own file already holds the April 2025 figures it refers to, roughly 40 per cent against roughly 20 per cent ongoing pregnancy versus standard IVM, and those are company-reported.

TALKING POINTS. What IVM is, in one plain sentence: maturing eggs outside the body rather than driving maturation with hormones inside it. Why that is a smaller category than IVF. Why "standard IVM" is therefore a narrow bar, and "standard IVF" would have been a much wider one. ON-AIR REFUSAL: I am not going to tell you whether the product is good. I have not seen the Phase 3 result and neither has anybody else.

SEGMENT 2: What the registry actually says (6 to 7 min). THE SPINE OF THE EPISODE.

SOURCE: ClinicalTrials.gov entry for NCT06858111, https://clinicaltrials.gov/study/NCT06858111 , registry updated 29 September 2026.

| What it says | Why it matters out loud |

|---|---|

| MediCult IVM listed as an ACTIVE COMPARATOR, classified in the registry as a device | An active comparator means the control arm receives a real competing product, not a placebo. That is a better trial in one sense and a narrower claim in another, and both halves get said. |

| The primary question is superiority over MediCult IVM | Not over conventional IVF. Everything downstream follows from this one line. |

| Randomised, parallel-group, quadruple-masked | Say what quadruple-masked means: participants, clinicians, investigators and assessors. This is a well-built trial and the episode should say so plainly. |

| Target enrolment about 500; a separate report says "more than 320" | LABELLED ON AIR AS UNRECONCILED. Plausibly enrolment to date against a 500 target, but neither source says so, so I am not going to claim it. |

| 11 sites, one country. Start 17 April 2025. Primary completion estimated May 2027 | The result does not exist yet. Anyone telling you how this turns out is guessing. |

EPISTEMIC LABEL: registry entries are self-reported by sponsors under a disclosure regime. They are vastly better than a press release and they are not peer review.

LABEL FOR THE COMPARATOR'S MAKER, AND IT IS A CAREFUL ONE. Trade coverage describes the comparator as CooperSurgical's Medicult IVM and as the first product of its kind to reach the market. The registry calls it a device without naming a manufacturer. Both can be true, and neither source states the link directly, so on air it is "a commercially available IVM system" and the manufacturer is not asserted.

SEGMENT 3: The general tool, which is the reason to listen (4 to 5 min)

STANCE: give them the question, not the verdict.

Three questions that fix almost any quoted improvement: superior to what, in whom, measured how.

  • Superior to what is the comparator. Today's worked example.
  • In whom is the population. This trial takes women 18 to 35 with AMH of at least 3 nanograms per millilitre, which is a selected group, and that is normal trial design rather than a flaw.
  • Measured how is the endpoint. The primary outcome here is cumulative ongoing pregnancy across up to the first two embryo transfers, and live birth is a secondary outcome. State it and move on. Do not editorialise it.

CLIP MOMENT: "Superior to what, in whom, measured how. If a number cannot survive those three questions, it was never a number about you."

SEGMENT 4: Why a storage company is the one saying this (2 to 3 min)

STANCE: declare the interest, then use it.

We sell storage. We have no position on whether this product works, no commercial exposure to the outcome, and nothing to gain from either result. That is exactly why we can read the registry out loud. ON-AIR, EXPLICIT: in vitro maturation is not in vitro gametogenesis. No mature human gametes have been produced by anyone. Fertilo is investigational in the United States with no FDA authorisation.

Then turn it inward, because that is this channel's line now: run the same three questions on us. Superior to what: we claim superiority to nothing. In whom: our claims are about samples, not people. Measured how: by a quality system a third party audits and publishes, which you can check on the accreditor's register rather than on our website.

OUTRO CTA

No product push. "The registry entry I read today is public, free, and took ten minutes. If somebody quotes you a number this week, go and find out what it is a number against. That is the whole skill." MySpawn storage is $99 a year, billed annually, and the founding annual rate is locked for the first 1,000 members.

THESIS: a cut-off is an administrative line drawn for somebody's purpose. It is not a statement about your body.

| Beat | Script direction | B-roll |

| 4 | The general move: every cut-off serves somebody's purpose. Trial entry. Insurance coverage. A lab's reference range. A regulator's threshold. | Four labelled cards. |

| 5 | "Here is the part that costs people something. | 6 | The men's example, concretely: a value just below a cut-off and a value just above it are nearly the same measurement, and they get two different words attached. Normal. Low. | Two near-identical numbers, different labels appearing. |
