SCRIPT 1: "One Name Or Two" (52 seconds, 40-60 read)

Script

[SHOT 1: static, a desk, a single sheet of paper face down. Hold 2 seconds.]

VO: "If you rent a safe deposit box, there is one detail that decides everything that happens after you die. It is not what is inside it."

[SHOT 2: slow push on the sheet.]

VO: "It is whether there is one name on it, or two."

[SHOT 3: cut to black, white text.]

VO: "Two names. A surviving co-renter can generally open it with a death certificate and photo identification. That is a morning."

[ON SCREEN TEXT: Two names: a death certificate and an ID.]

[SHOT 4: hold black, second line.]

VO: "One name. Now it runs through the estate, and the bank wants the court's own paperwork naming whoever is entitled to act."

[ON SCREEN TEXT: One name: the court's paperwork.]

[SHOT 5: the desk again, the sheet still face down.]

VO: "How long that takes depends entirely on which state you are in. Some let a family member in on a sworn affidavit. Others want a petition filed with a court. Both are normal. Only one of them is fast."

[SHOT 6: a hand turns the sheet over. It is blank.]

VO: "Here is the part worth four minutes of your week. If your bank offers a joint renter or a deputy, adding one converts the second version into the first."

[SHOT 7: static, the blank sheet.]

VO: "And write down what is in the box, and keep that list somewhere that is not the box. A list inside the box is a joke the bank has heard before."

[SHOT 8: cut to black.]

VO: "We store a DNA record. Ninety-nine dollars a year, billed annually. Different object, same question: who can get it, and what do they have to show."

Sound: room tone only until shot 6, then one low sustained note. No music under the two-column beat; the contrast does the work.
