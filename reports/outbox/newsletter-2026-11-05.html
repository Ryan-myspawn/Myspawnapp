PLAINTEXT VERSION

THE LEAD: the word that breaks the sentence

In June, researchers used base editing on human embryos: a single chemical swap of one letter, rather than the older method that cuts the strand and lets the cell repair it. They found a gene called NANOG is required for the embryo to form the tissue that becomes the body. That is a peer-reviewed result, published in Nature in June 2026, and the precision is real.

The interesting part is in the coverage that followed. In some embryos, the edit took in some cells and not in others. There is a word for that: mosaicism.

Sit with what it does to a sentence we have all read for ten years. "The edit worked."

Worked in what? An embryo is not one thing. It is a few dozen cells, and after an edit like this they are no longer all carrying the same genome. One group becomes the body, and nobody knows at the time which.

So "it worked" hides at least three different numbers: the share of cells carrying the edit, the share of embryos where anything was edited, and the share of embryos where every single cell carried it. Only the third would matter clinically.

We went looking for that third number. We could not find it. Four separate pieces of coverage discuss mosaicism in this work and none prints a rate. So we are not going to estimate it, call it low, or call it high.

One more fact, and it is the history of this field in a sentence. In earlier mouse work, losing this gene disrupted two tissues. In the human embryos, it affected mainly one. The mouse result did not transfer.

THREE THINGS WORTH KNOWING

Science. There are four routes in the United States to a drug not approved for the use you want. Off-label prescribing, where the drug is approved for something else and the agency states it has not assessed the new use. Expanded access, the formal name for compassionate use. The Right to Try Act of 2018, which removes the agency review step. And a clinical trial. One reported figure reframes the middle two: the FDA authorises more than 99 per cent of expanded access requests, generally within hours or days. The review was mostly not the obstacle. And under either investigational route, the company is under no obligation to supply anything.

Culture. Prepaying for a service is lending. If the company fails you are usually an unsecured creditor, paid last. A narrow consumer priority exists in bankruptcy, capped in the low thousands, and it covers only what was never delivered. Some industries are required by state law to hold prepayments in escrow: health clubs in several states, funeral preneed sellers. We could not find a state that requires it of genetic storage companies.

Product, and it is an admission. We cannot yet point you to a published document saying what would happen to your stored sample if MySpawn stopped operating: who you would contact, what you would have to prove, how long it would take. Your sample is physically held by a separate company, which helps, but a structural separation is not a written continuity route. We are writing that document. Until it is published, the honest answer is: not yet.
