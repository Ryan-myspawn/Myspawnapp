## THE POSITIONING, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

**The useful question is not what it costs. It is how many prices it has.**

A service can honestly have four: an annual fee, a larger up-front figure that lowers the annual fee, a prepaid plan covering a fixed term, and a prepaid plan covering all of it. **All four can be real and none of them is a lie.** They serve different families with different problems, and offering four is more flexible than offering one. **But it means the number you met was chosen for you by a landing page.**

**Our answer is one.** $99 a year, billed annually, first 1,000 members lock the founding rate. **No prepaid tier, no term plan, no lifetime option, no promotional percentage.** That is not a virtue we invented; **it was invisible to us until we went and counted somebody else's prices**, which is exactly why it makes a good ad and a bad boast.

### BINDING RAILS ON THIS BATCH

1. **NO COMPETITOR IS NAMED, PRICED, COUNTED OR ALLUDED TO.** Every figure behind this week's finding is **search-index material from pages our container could not open**, and **today we corrected a published range of our own that turned out not to contain one of the three providers it named.** **We do not get to publish a number about somebody else on the same day we widen one of our own.** Nothing in this batch states how many prices anyone else has. It states the question, and our answer.
2. **A MULTI-RUNG PRICE LADDER IS NOT DISHONEST, AND NO ASSET MAY IMPLY IT IS.** Prepaid plans are a legitimate product for families who want renewal risk off the table. **The contrast is that we have nothing to explain, not that anyone is hiding anything.**
3. **THEREFORE "HIDDEN" IS RETIRED FROM THE PRICING LANE.** Ad_RealNumber_44's artwork line **"NO HIDDEN TIERS"** implies concealment and is **not a claim we can support about anybody.** **It does not appear in this batch and should not be reused.** The honest form is **"no tiers"**, which is a statement about us alone.
4. **STORAGE CONFERS NO BENEFIT BEYOND STORAGE.** No health claim, no treatment claim, no descendant claim, in either direction.

## (a) META: three concepts, one per audience

### CONCEPT M1 - Audience: 25-40, future-optionality
- **Primary text (lead):** *Ask how many prices it has. Not how much. How many.*
- **Longer variant:** *Ask how many prices it has. Not how much. How many. A service can have an annual fee, an up-front option, a fixed-term prepaid plan and a lifetime plan, all real, all live at the same time, and the one you meet depends on the page you landed on. MySpawn has one. $99/year, billed annually, and the first 1,000 members lock the founding rate. Storage of a DNA record, held by an accredited third-party biorepository. That is the whole price list.*
- **Headline:** How Many Prices Does It Have?
- **Description:** $99/year, billed annually
- **CTA button:** Learn more
- **Visual direction:** a single printed line on an otherwise empty page, shot flat and cold. **No price card, no glow, no receipt**: those are Ad_44's and Ad_ItemizedReceipt_31's, and this concept's subject is emptiness rather than proof.

### CONCEPT M2 - Audience: 40-60, family history and mortality salience
- **Primary text (lead):** *We have one price. There is no second one waiting for you in year eleven.*
- **Longer variant:** *We have one price. There is no second one waiting for you in year eleven. This matters more than it sounds, because the person who eventually deals with a renewal may not be you, and "why is this bill different now" is a bad question to leave behind. $99/year, billed annually. One number, stated the same way every year, with no tier to move up into and nothing to prepay.*
- **Headline:** No Second Price In Year Eleven
- **Description:** One number, every year
- **CTA button:** Sign up
- **Visual direction:** a plain unopened window envelope on a wooden table, morning light, shot wide and cool. **No calendar, no clock, no hourglass.**

### CONCEPT M3 - Audience: childfree legacy
- **Primary text (lead):** *Nobody is going to inherit your paperwork. That is a design constraint.*
- **Longer variant:** *Nobody is going to inherit your paperwork. That is a design constraint, not a sad fact, and it argues for a price you can still hold in your head in thirty years. One number a year, no tier structure to re-learn and no prepaid term to remember the end of. $99/year, billed annually. You pick who the record is for, and you are allowed to change your mind about that later.*
- **Headline:** A Price You Can Hold In Your Head
- **Description:** A number you can remember
- **CTA button:** Learn more
- **Visual direction:** a single sheet of paper in a wire tray, everything else empty, shot dark. **No family imagery of any kind and no empty chair.**
- **BINDING TONE NOTE:** **the lead line must read as practical, never as consolation.** It says administration does not get handed on; **it does not say anything about whether that is a loss.** **If a reviewer hears pity in it, kill the concept rather than soften it.**
