Title: "An Input Is Not An Outcome"

NEW AXIS, AND IT IS THE FOURTH DISTINCT VERIFICATION AXIS rather than a repeat. 24 October asked whether the evidence EXISTS. 25 October asked WHO it was measured on. 27 October asked whether the ADDRESS given for the evidence is real. CROSS-LANE DEPENDENCY, DECLARED IN ADVANCE PER THE 25 OCTOBER RULE. This morning's radar proposed a short-form concept, "Seventy Against Fifty-Two", on the same figures. The Content Factory should build that short FROM this explainer or skip it, and must say which in its own file. The long form is here; two lanes writing the same numbers independently is the collision that rule exists to prevent.

Spine. An input measure tells you a step worked. An outcome measure tells you the thing you wanted happened. Most of the distance between a promising technology and a useful one lives in that gap, and most marketing lives there too.

Worked example, with every label spoken on air. Phase 1 and Phase 2 results for the maturation therapy in the news exist as a PREPRINT, which means not peer-reviewed. In the Phase 2 comparison against standard in vitro maturation, the treated group reportedly had 70 per cent mature eggs against 52 per cent. That is a maturation rate. It counts how many eggs finished maturing. It does not count pregnancies and it does not count births. It is also a sponsor comparison, and it is not the Phase 3 result.

B-roll directions. (1) Two empty measuring jugs, one labelled by a hand-written card reading STEP and one reading RESULT, filled to different levels. No third-party packaging in frame. (2) A close static shot of a page of text with one line highlighted, no legible content, shallow depth. (3) Presenter at a plain table, no set dressing. (4) A card holding 70 and 52 for a full four seconds with the preprint label UNDER the numbers, not beside them. Binding: if the label is cropped out in the edit, the video is pulled, because it would then be committing the error it describes.

The general rule, stated so it outlives this example. Ask three questions of any number offered as progress. What did it count? Who counted it? And is the thing it counted the thing I would pay for? A maturation rate is a real measurement and a real achievement. It is an input. Phase 3 exists precisely because inputs do not reliably predict outcomes, which is also why the trial's own comparator is the existing method rather than nothing.

Preserve-now close, without overpromising. No claim that any of this is purchasable. "None of this is something we sell. What we do is keep a sample of what already exists, at ambient temperature, under contract, for ninety-nine dollars a year, billed annually. That is an outcome we can describe without a trial, because the claim is small enough to be checked."

On air, stated: this script cites one preprint, no peer-reviewed study, and no source opened at its own domain. Thirty-sixth consecutive checked day.
