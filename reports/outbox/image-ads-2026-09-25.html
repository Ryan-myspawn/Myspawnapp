The structural finding, which is worth more than the shortfall costs

Two days, two Content-Factory-briefed consumer objects, two total wipeouts: hairbrush 8 for 8 yesterday, cast iron 6 for 6 today. The cause is diagnosable: consumer objects are photographed overwhelmingly by the people who sell them. The supply for a hairbrush, a frying pan or a stove handle is brand product shoots, 3D renders and food styling. Every photo shipped this week was a non-consumer subject: a leaf, antique luggage, a museum sundial, stone steps, knitwear, ice.

Proposed brief rule, costs no founder time and is not adopted unilaterally: when a script names a household object as its device, the AD carries the idea on a subject nobody sells. That one change would have saved both wipeouts.
