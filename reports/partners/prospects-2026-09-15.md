## What changed in the template this week, and why it matters

Previous weeks' drafts stated the GenVault credentials as a flat list. **This week's version
attributes them explicitly and de-claims "FDA-registered" in the same sentence**, because:
- The credentials belong to **GenVault**, and blurring that in a letter to lawyers is the kind of
  small imprecision that costs the whole relationship.
- **FDA registration is not FDA approval.** Per the agency's own position, a registration number
  means the facility is registered and conveys no approval or endorsement. We published an article
  and an ad on exactly this yesterday; sending outreach that leans on the phrase while our own blog
  explains it is a low bar, and we should clear it.

**Recommendation: retrofit this wording into the three prior channel templates** (estate attorneys
week 1, financial advisors week 2, genetic counselors week 3) before any of them is ever sent. They
are all still unsent, so this costs nothing now and would cost credibility later.
