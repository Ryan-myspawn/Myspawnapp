- **Dominant format this week:** ultra-short and unpolished. TikTok is actively boosting 7-second videos; Instagram's own data shows single-take content beating studio production. ### 1. NEWSJACK (do this first): "The military just started paying for what we do"
**Platform:** TikTok + Reels (talking-head — your Reel 12/13 format). **Trend:** Pentagon pilot reimburses troops up to $10K/yr for egg freezing, $500/yr for sperm cryopreservation before deployment ([Stars and Stripes, Aug 13](https://www.stripes.com/theaters/us/2026-08-13/freeze-eggs-sperm-before-deployment-separation-22540351.html)).
**Hook (0-2s):** "The U.S. military will now pay soldiers $10,000 to preserve their DNA before deployment."
**Beats:** (1) hook over the headline on screen → (2) "not a conspiracy — it starts September 14" → (3) "the Pentagon understands something most people don't: preservation can only happen while you're still here" → (4) "soldiers get it funded. Civilians get told it's weird." → (5) "$99. Seven minutes. Same principle." → CTA.
**Caption:** The Pentagon now funds biological preservation for troops. MySpawn is the civilian version. $99, first 1,000 members.
**Hashtags:** #military #dnapreservation #pentagon #legacy #biotech #futureproof #myspawn
**Brand fit:** validates your entire premise with a government precedent, and dovetails with your existing soldier series. **Risk:** keep it respectful of service members; no politics, just the policy fact.
