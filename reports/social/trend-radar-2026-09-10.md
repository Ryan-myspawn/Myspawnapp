## Science Watch
### NEWLY DISCOVERED — Paterna Biosciences, lab-grown human sperm (May 13, 2026)
- **What they claim:** starting from a small testicular biopsy, they matured spermatogonial stem cells through spermatogenesis in vitro, producing sperm they describe as structurally and genetically indistinguishable from in-body sperm, then used them to create early test embryos "solely as preliminary validation."
- **Epistemic label: COMPANY ANNOUNCEMENT.** Not published in a peer-reviewed journal, not independently replicated, not cleared for clinical use. Coverage (BioSpace/PRNewswire release, Gizmodo, Newsweek) is reporting the claim, not verifying it.
- **THE PRECISION FIX (act on this before the next episode):** our standing on-air line is "IVG has produced no mature human gametes." That remains accurate for **IVG in the strict sense** — gametes derived from induced pluripotent stem cells. Paterna's claim is **in vitro spermatogenesis from existing testicular stem cells**, which is a different starting material and a different technical problem. Going forward we should say: *"No lab has produced a mature human gamete from an iPSC. One company claims in vitro maturation of a man's own testicular stem cells, unpublished and unreplicated."* Sloppier phrasing than that is now falsifiable by a motivated commenter.
- **So what (preserve-now angle):** none, and we should say so. This is a fertility-treatment claim for men with certain infertility, not a legacy product, and it has zero bearing on record storage. If anything it reinforces the rail: we store records, we do not touch gametes.
