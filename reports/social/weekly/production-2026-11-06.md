## 1. EVERGREEN BANK: one new idea

### "You Stopped Hearing It" (about 30 seconds)

**NEW MECHANISM: THE SCREEN STAYS BLACK FOR THE FIRST HALF.** The piece opens with sound and no image, and the image does not arrive until the sound has done its work. **Every item in this bank to date is a visual demonstration with sound as decoration. This one inverts that.** **`audio first`, `screen stays black`, `sound before` and `no picture` all return ZERO across the weekly, scripts and ads directories. `black frame` returns 4 in scripts and all four are cut-to-black instructions inside other pieces, not a format, which is declared rather than claimed clean.**

**Beats:**
1. **Black frame. No image. A steady low hum begins: a fridge, a vent, a server cabinet, anything ordinary and continuous.** No caption, no logo, nothing.
2. The hum runs, unchanged, for a long time by short-form standards. **Twenty seconds.** Nothing happens.
3. **The hum cuts out.** Silence, two full seconds, still black.
4. The image arrives for the first time: **an ordinary room, nothing in it, no person, locked off.**
5. One card: **"You stopped hearing it after about four seconds."**
6. Cut. No voice at any point, no music, no price, no URL.

**Why it is the right evergreen for this pack:** **it is a demonstration rather than a claim, it performs on the viewer rather than at them, and the viewer verifies it against their own experience of the previous twenty seconds.** **It is also the only piece in the bank whose subject is not noticing, which is the whole custody argument stated without a single word about custody.**

**BINDING CONDITIONS:**
- **The hum must be real room sound, recorded in one take with the image.** **If it is a library loop the piece is dropped**, because the claim in the card is about what just happened to the viewer and a fake sound makes it a trick.
- **Twenty seconds is a floor, not a target.** If it tests short the piece does not work, and the honest response is to drop it rather than cut it to fifteen.
- **No person, no voice, no object that identifies a place.**
- **Nothing is written anywhere except the single card.**

**PRODUCTION NOTE: this costs one take, one phone, one room and no performance, and the first half needs no picture at all.** **It is the second consecutive evergreen chosen partly because it can be shot today.**
