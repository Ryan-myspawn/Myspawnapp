### Segment 1 — The pressure machine
- **Quote (verbatim):** "Doctors are essentially being shoved in the direction of using it, not because they want to, but because they will lose patients if they don't." — Orchid founder Noor Siddiqui, August 2026 online event, as reported by Entrepreneur Loop: https://entrepreneurloop.com/us-fertility-startup-controversy-over-genetic-optimization/
- **Stance:** react to the dynamic, not the person; quote exactly. FOMO-driven clinical adoption is how categories break trust: the customer pipeline outruns the evidence pipeline.
- **Receipts:** Nucleus's first clinic partner (Beverly Hills Fertility) and its expansion to India and Middle East clinics (Bloomberg, Mar 26, 2026) show the same commercial acceleration.
- **CLIP MOMENT:** "When the founder says doctors are being shoved, believe her."

### Segment 2 — Grading your own homework
- **Facts:** the Psychiatric Genomics Consortium, 800+ researchers, says Orchid used its data in violation of screening restrictions (Science/AAAS: https://www.science.org/content/article/genetics-group-slams-company-using-its-data-screen-embryos-genomes). This month Orchid scientists posted a bioRxiv preprint concluding their own five-cell biopsy method is accurate. **Label on air: company-authored preprint, not peer-reviewed.**
- **Stance:** a preprint is not evidence of wrongdoing, it's normal science communication. The problem is sequence: validation AFTER commercialization, authored by the seller, while the data's own creators object. That's grading your own homework mid-controversy.
- **Talking points:** teach the 90-second referee test: (1) who validated this, seller or independents? (2) who profits if you believe it? (3) is the data where critics can check it? Works on embryo screens, supplements, and longevity clinics alike.
- **CLIP MOMENT:** the referee test, delivered in one breath.

- **One contextual line only:** and this is the same industry neighborhood where 15 million genomes just went through a bankruptcy auction and a "forever" memorial app died in a year; long-promise categories keep failing on the trust dimension, not the science dimension.
- **CLIP MOMENT:** "The science is a sliver. The certainty is the product."

### Segment 4 — Trust as the product (close)
