- **Stance:** refuse the average, on air. **"If I average five numbers from five unlike sources I produce a sixth number that nobody has ever been charged. That is not analysis. That is arithmetic doing a costume change."**

## Segment 2: What a published price actually is (4-5 min)
- **The argument:** a published price is not primarily information about cost. **It is a commitment that the same thing costs the same to everyone**, and it is checkable by a stranger in four seconds.
- **The inversion:** an unpublished price transfers the work of discovery onto the buyer, and it makes the buyer's leverage a variable. **Talking points: who gets the good price, and what did they have that the next person does not.**
- **Honest counterweight, said plainly so the segment is not a hit piece:** bespoke pricing is normal and often legitimate in medicine, in law, and in anything genuinely bespoke. **"I am not claiming it is sinister. I am claiming it is information about you, not about them."**
