## 3. EVERGREEN BANK (new format)

**Format: "Read It Out Loud."** A single unbroken take, one page of a real consumer terms-of-service document on the desk, read aloud verbatim, no commentary until the final ten seconds. **Format not used in the last 7 days** (recent: mirror-shot 12th, whiteboard 14th, wall-calendar 15th, Flop-Core self-correction 16th, retail-aisle-walk 17th). Closest prior relatives are the group-chat screen-scroll (29 Aug) and the document macro demo (26 Aug), both well outside the window and both visually different.

- **Hook (first 2 seconds):** "I am going to read you one paragraph and add nothing to it."
- **Beats:**
  1. Page on the desk, finger on the clause. No face for the first fifteen seconds.
  2. Read the retention clause verbatim. Slowly. Do not editorialise, do not sigh, do not raise an eyebrow.
  3. Read the second clause: the one distinguishing deleting your results from destroying your sample.
  4. Silence. Two full seconds.
  5. To camera: "That is the whole video. I did not characterise it. I read it. You can find it in eleven minutes and you should, for whichever company has your stuff."
  6. Close: "Ask the same question of us. We publish where the samples are and who stores them."
- **Why it works for men 25-55:** no sentiment, no fear, no music. The format is a demonstration of a habit rather than an argument.
- **Sourcing rule, binding:** the clause read on camera must be **screen-captured from the live document on the day of filming**, with the company name visible only if the reading is exact. **If there is any doubt about currency, film a redacted version and name the category, not the company.** Our own 16 September article is the research basis, not the on-camera source.
