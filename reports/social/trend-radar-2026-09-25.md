### WEEKLY NAMED-PLATFORM DIGITAL-LEGACY SWEEP (due today, run today)

**One new fact, one live conflict, and one confirmation that our archive was already right.**

**NEW, and it is a gap in a published article of ours.** Multiple 2026 guides state that **an Apple Legacy Contact can access a deceased user's account data for three years, after which Apple deletes the account.** **Our own `platform-legacy-settings-explained.md` documents how to nominate a Legacy Contact and the OS requirements, and says nothing about a time limit.** **If the three-year window is real it is the single most actionable fact on that page and it is missing.**
**Epistemic label: secondary sources only, not Apple documentation, and not verified.** **Action: verify against Apple's own support pages before adding it, and do not use it in any asset until then.**

**CONFIRMED, already on file:** **Google's Inactive Account Manager waits 3 to 18 months** by user setting, which our article already states correctly, including the optional deletion three months after inactivity is declared. Today's sweep adds that **Google reserves the right to delete accounts inactive for at least two years** with prior warnings. **Also secondary-sourced; treat as a separate claim from the Inactive Account Manager timer, because they are different mechanisms.**

**CONFIRMED, no change:** **Meta memorializes on a valid request, has no inactivity deletion, and a legacy contact cannot read private messages or alter previous posts.**

**THE CONFLICT, and the reason this section is worth running weekly.** One 2026 guide states flatly that **TikTok offers no memorialization and no advance legacy planning, and that the only family action is requesting deletion.** **If true, longpost #77 ("The Person You Name Is Not Allowed to Open Anything", 19 September) and the 18 September episode are both built on a feature that does not exist.**

**It is not true, and it took one search to establish that.** TikTok prompts users to **"Choose someone you trust to manage your account after you pass away"**, and that legacy contact can **update the bio, accept new friend requests, download copies of posts, manage privacy settings and request deactivation.** Users can instead choose **"Delete after death."** **That is our restriction list, and #77 stands.**

**What IS genuinely contested is memorialization specifically**, which is a different feature from a legacy contact: some sources describe a "Remembering" label with nobody able to log in, others say TikTok has no memorialized state at all. **We are not asserting either. Nothing in the archive depends on it.**
