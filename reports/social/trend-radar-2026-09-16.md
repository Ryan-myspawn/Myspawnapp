## Science Watch

**NEW TO THIS RADAR, AND HANDLED CAREFULLY: microplastics and the blood-testis barrier.**

The surfaced 2026 material:
- **"Mechanisms of Polystyrene Microplastics in Multipathway Disruption of the Blood-Testis Barrier,"** Liu et al., *Andrology*, 2026 (DOI 10.1111/andr.70296). **Peer-reviewed.** Reports that PS-MP exposure increased blood-testis barrier permeability in a dose- and time-dependent manner.
- A 2026 *Journal of Hazardous Materials* paper reporting that polystyrene microplastics internalise into **Sertoli cells**, trigger CEBPB-mediated lysosomal hyperactivation, degrade tight-junction proteins, and induce ferroptosis-like injury in human sperm. **Peer-reviewed.**
- A rodent study reporting dose-dependent declines in sperm count and motility, more abnormalities, and reduced testosterone at low polystyrene doses. **Animal model.**
- The widely circulated human figure: microplastics detected in **68%** of testicular tissue samples analysed, with those samples showing a mean sperm count of **12 million/mL** versus **26 million/mL** where none were detected. **This figure predates 2026 and is frequently recirculated as new. Date it before any use.**

**So what, and the rail comes first.** MySpawn stores records. We do not address fertility, we do not fix sperm counts, and nothing here may be used to imply otherwise. **The only approved use is a zero-product science explainer.** There is a second, sharper reason to be careful: the same coverage notes there is **no conclusive evidence that microplastics directly cause human infertility, and no proof that sauna-based "detox" protocols remove microplastics from sperm or improve fertility.** That combination, a real mechanism plus a fake remedy, is the exact shape of the hype we exist to push back on.

**Action before use: date every item.** None of the four is confirmed inside a 14-day window, and one is a known recirculator. Added to the watchlist with the rail attached.
