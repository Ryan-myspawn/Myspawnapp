### 2. STALE-ITEM CATCH #17, and it is the first one this series has caught me creating

The IVG sweep was scoped, as always, to the window. The answer came back under the heading **"Key September 2026 Announcement"**. The fact underneath that heading was **Conception's late-June 2026 primary-oocyte announcement**, which this file has carried since June and **already logged as stale-item catch #11 on 17 September.**

**Here is what is new, and it is uncomfortable.** Nothing in the source material was wrong or misdated. The company's page is honest. The June date is not hidden anywhere. **The "September 2026" heading did not come from a publisher. It came from the summarizer, and it came from my own query, which had "September 2026" in it.**

**NEW FAILURE MODE: a date-scoped query can induce a date-shaped heading over an undated fact.** The scoping term is echoed back as framing. This is **more dangerous than both earlier modes**, because with anniversary dates (#16) and undated statistics the misleading element is at least present in the source, where a careful read finds it. **Here the misleading element is generated at read time, by me, and it will therefore appear on exactly the items I most want to be new.**

**Check added to the sweep, effective now: when a summary's heading contains the date I searched for, treat the heading as mine until a primary source restates the date.** This joins **21 Sep** (read the full description, not the thumbnail), **22 Sep** (read the description for provenance as well as content), **23 Sep** (read for medium: 3D renders are indexed as photographs) and **23 Sep** (anniversary dates outrank years in date-scoped search).
