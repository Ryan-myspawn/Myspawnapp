**The two corrections that make the segment worth making:**

2. **"fertility rates are down 62%."** **Meaningless without a baseline.** Global TFR has fallen by roughly half since the 1950s; US TFR went from about **2.08 in 1990 to about 1.6 in 2024**, which is nearer 23%. **Ask which number, over which period, for which population.** *(Our own US TFR figures are already on file from the IFS entry.)*
3. **"42% over forty have erectile..."** the classic reference point is the Massachusetts Male Aging Study finding that roughly **half of men aged 40 to 70** reported some degree of erectile dysfunction. **The claim is in the right neighbourhood and still needs its source named.**
