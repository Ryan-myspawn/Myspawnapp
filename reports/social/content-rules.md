# BINDING RULE — Content Agents (Effective 2026-08-21, issued by founder)
*Every content agent (Trend Radar, SEO Writer, Longpost Studio, Deep Production, Content Factory AM/PM, Image Ad Creative, Ad Copy Lab, Newsletter Writer) must obey this before producing anything. It overrides older lane instructions where they conflict.*

## 1. Zero Tolerance for Repetition
Forbidden to repeat the same core ideas, angles, or formats across days.
Specifically banned or heavily restricted:
- "100-year letter" or ANY letter-to-the-future framing (outdated — users prefer VIDEO messages in the Vault, Interstellar-style; use video-message framing instead)
- Pentagon / military DNA / government biobank stories (overused)
- Any idea that has appeared in the last 7 days of outputs
Before proposing any idea, check: "Has this exact angle, emotional hook, or format been used in the last week?" If yes, discard and generate something new.

## 2. Forced Freshness Protocol
Every day:
- Lead with a current real-world trend, news story, scientific paper, or cultural moment from the last 7-14 days
- Connect that trend to MySpawn in a new way
- Prefer timely over evergreen
If no strong timely hook exists, say so and propose 2 alternative fresh directions instead of recycling old ones.

## 3. Men-Primary Focus
MySpawn currently converts better with men. All content written primarily for men aged 25-55, while remaining respectful and open to women.
Preferred emotional territory: legacy / bloodline / "what remains of me"; control and optionality in an uncertain future; quiet strength and responsibility; technology as a tool, not a fantasy; status of being early / foresight others lack; practical realism over emotional storytelling.
Avoid or minimize: soft, overly sentimental, or "dear future child" framing; heavy focus on motherhood or female reproductive anxiety; letter-writing or diary-style nostalgia.
(Note: on Reddit, "bloodline" language remains banned per reddit-playbook.md; the playbook wins there.)

## 4. Preferred Content Directions (rotate)
- Future reproductive technology (IVG, synthetic gametes) and what it means for men
- Billionaire / elite access gap (NEW examples only)
- Real scientific progress in biobanking or longevity that happened this week
- Cultural moments (movies, sports, tech news) tied to biological legacy
- Practical "why now" urgency without fear-mongering
- Status and foresight framing ("Most men will leave nothing. A few will leave an option.")

## 5. Output Requirement
Top of every content suggestion:
**Freshness check:** This angle has not been used in the last 7 days.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** [specific recent trend or news]
If you cannot honestly write that line, do not output the idea.
