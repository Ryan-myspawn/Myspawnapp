## Trends (delta only, last 24-48h)

**1. Q1 2026 longevity-biotech financing: the only clean number of the week.**

**49 financing events between 1 January and 30 March 2026. 41 of them disclosed a size. Those 41 total approximately $3.74 billion, a 56 percent increase over Q1 2025, with deal count up from 43 to 49.** Analysis credited to **Longevity.Technology**, built on **PitchBook** data, and picked up by Forbes.

**Why this one is different from everything the lane produced this month.** It has a **date range**, a **denominator** (41 of 49, and it says so), a **method** (disclosed transaction sizes, summed), and a **publisher with a named data source**. Every generational-wealth figure logged on 24 September had none of those.

**What it is not.** It is **not a market size**. It is money that changed hands in a quarter, which is a receipt. A market-size figure is a model of how large something could be, which is a forecast. **The 24 September rail bans market-size figures in MySpawn assets and that rail stands; it does not reach this number.** But two of the three dating questions do apply: **when was this measured** (nine months of the year are now gone) and **who publishes it** (a longevity-industry outlet has an interest in the number being large, which is not an accusation and is a disclosure).

**Usable, with conditions.** If any asset cites it, it carries the date range, the 41-of-49 denominator, and the publisher. **It never gets rounded to "nearly $4 billion" and it never gets annualized.**

**2. Generational wealth: twelve quiet days end, with a headline we should not use and a gap we should.**

Fortune, **22 September**: the claim that Gen Z is financially better off than any generation before it at the same age. Figures attached, all as reported by that piece:

- Millennials at age 34 held an average net worth of **$331,000**, against **$251,000** for Gen X and **$229,000** for Boomers at the same age.
- Gen Z respondents name **$9.5 million** as the threshold of financial success.
- **62 percent** aspire to retire comfortably; **46 percent** believe they actually will.
- **60 percent** of companies reportedly let Gen Z hires go within their first few months in 2026.

**Rails before use.** These are **averages, not medians**, which for net worth is the difference between a description of a generation and a description of its top decile. The **$9.5 million** figure is a survey of aspiration, not of holdings. The **60 percent** hiring figure is a survey of employers and is the softest number in the set. **None of them is independently verified here, and no asset states any of them as a fact about the world; they are stated as what one dated article reports.**

**The asset is the 62-versus-46 gap.** Sixteen points between what people hope for and what they expect. That gap is the entire emotional content of this lane and it needs no dollar figure at all. It is also the first thing in twelve days in this lane that is a **human sentence rather than a trillion-dollar number nobody dated.**

**What we still will not do.** No claim that MySpawn is a financial product, an inheritance vehicle, or a retirement input. The adjacency is that people who think about what they hand on rarely arrange it; that is the whole of it.
