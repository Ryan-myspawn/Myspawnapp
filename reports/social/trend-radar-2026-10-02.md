## Trends (delta only, last 24-48h)

**THE DELTA: "LEGACY CONTACT" IS ONE PHRASE COVERING THREE INCOMPATIBLE POWERS.**

- **Facebook.** A legacy contact can post to the timeline, respond to friend requests, update the profile and cover photo, and download an archive of photos and posts. **They cannot log in as the deceased user and cannot access private messages.** Users can alternatively ask for permanent deletion at death. ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/social-media/facebook-legacy-contact-runs-your-profile-after-you-die-n305086), [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/control-what-happens-to-your-facebook-after-you-die).)
- **Apple.** Up to five legacy contacts, who need **a pre-shared access key plus a death certificate**. Reported access to photos, messages, notes and files, **for up to three years from the first approved request, after which the account and its data are permanently deleted.** ([Nixon Peabody](https://www.nixonpeabody.com/insights/articles/2022/06/07/create-a-legacy-contact-for-your-apple-id), [Digital Death Directory, University of Manitoba](https://digitaldeathdirectory.cs.umanitoba.ca/platforms/apple).)
- **Ancestry.** A designated legacy contact who, **on verification of a qualifying event such as death, receives full account access and assumes ownership of the account including all personal information contained within it.** ([Provision record of the Ancestry privacy statement](https://conductatlas.com/platform/ancestry/ancestry-privacy-statement/provision/CA-P-014884/legacy-contact-and-account-transfer/).)
