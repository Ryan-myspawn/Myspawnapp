# MySpawn Science Watchlist
*Standing knowledge base for the daily Trend Radar. Last full revision: 2026-08-17. Update entries in place when funding/trials/papers change; add a dated line to the Update Log.*

## Why this file exists
MySpawn's core narrative for the 25-60 audience: **the science that could one day use a preserved sample is moving fast, and preservation is the only step that can't be taken retroactively.** Every watchlist item below is a potential "front row seat" story. Accuracy is the moat: competitors will overhype; we newsjack with receipts.

**Hard accuracy rules:** (1) MySpawn is storage only: never promise descendant creation. (2) IVG has produced NO mature human gametes; no human born from a lab-made gamete. (3) Artificial wombs: partial ectogenesis (premature-infant rescue) is real and near-term; full ectogenesis (conception to birth outside a body) is speculative, decades out. (4) Always label: peer-reviewed vs company press release vs rumor.

## Storage science (the "it works" proof points)
- **World-record embryo:** baby born **July 26, 2025** from an embryo frozen **~31 years** (created 1994; MIT Tech Review exclusive July 29, 2025; Guinness-certified): vitrified samples may be viable indefinitely.
- **Ambient-temperature DNA:** ensilication study (*NAR Mol Med*, Oct 2025): room-temp DNA storage matched cryo, and frozen samples showed MORE artifactual mutations. Directly relevant to GenVault's ambient model.
- Caveat to keep straight: DNA-as-record preservation ≠ viable-cell preservation; gametes/embryos still need cryo.
