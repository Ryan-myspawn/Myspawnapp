  6. The honest MySpawn position: we don't tell anyone to have kids. We keep options and stories from expiring while you decide. Storage, not persuasion.
  7. Land it: "the gap that's actually measurable isn't between wanted and actual kids — it's between what people mean to preserve and what they get around to."
- **🎬 30-second closing take (clip-able):** "Pronatalists want you scared of the birth rate. Their critics want you relaxed about it.
