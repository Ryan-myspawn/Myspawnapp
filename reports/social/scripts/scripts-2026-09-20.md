## SCRIPT 1: "Two Glasses" (~70s)

**Freshness check:** No script in the archive uses a swap demonstration. Today's Deep Production explainer teaches the same distinction over six to ten minutes with drawn B-roll; **this does it physically in seventy seconds and never mentions a study by name.** Formats inside the 7-day window checked and excluded: settings walkthrough, eye chart, Black Screen, Overlay Audit, Cards Face Down, Read It Out Loud, Backwards From Five, The Ladder, One Object Three Owners.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** "Testosterone is dropping one percent a year" is circulating again, and there are two different one-percent findings being collapsed into one.

**Format:** two identical glasses of water on a table, top-down then eye-level. Hands only.

| # | [SHOT] | VO / on-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [SHOT: two identical full glasses, side by side.] | **VO:** "You have heard that men's testosterone is falling about one percent a year. That is true. There is a second finding, also about one percent, that is also true, and they are not the same thing." |
| 2 | [SHOT: hand pours a little out of the LEFT glass, three times, slowly.] | **[ON SCREEN: ONE MAN, OVER TIME]** **VO:** "This is the first one. One man, getting older. The level drifts down a little each year from somewhere around his thirties. That is ageing, and it is happening inside one person." |
| 3 | [SHOT: hand lifts the RIGHT glass away entirely and sets down a different, already-lower glass in its place.] | **[ON SCREEN: A DIFFERENT MAN, SAME AGE, LATER DECADE]** **VO:** "This is the second one. Nobody poured anything out. This is a different man, the same age, measured years later." |
| 4 | [SHOT: hold on the swapped glass.] | **VO:** "A fifty-year-old measured in the late eighties had a higher level than a comparable fifty-year-old measured in the mid nineties. Same age. Lower number. Later decade." |
| 5 | [SHOT: hands still.] | **VO:** "And when researchers controlled for illness, general health, medications, smoking, weight, work and marital status, that gap stayed almost exactly where it was." |
| 6 | [SHOT: both glasses in frame.] | **[ON SCREEN: BOTH ARE REAL. THEY POINT DIFFERENT WAYS.]** **VO:** "So which one is somebody quoting at you? If it is the first, the story is you are running out of time, and something is usually for sale at the end of it." |
| 7 | [SHOT: hand taps the swapped glass.] | **VO:** "If it is the second, the story is that something changed between those decades that nobody has pinned down. That is a research problem. Nothing you buy this week touches it." |
| 8 | [SHOT: hands withdraw.] | **VO:** "I am not a doctor and this is not advice about your body. If you want your number, the test is cheap and a physician can read it. What I am giving you is a question: is that a number about one person over time, or about a whole population across decades?" |

**Sound/music:** water. Nothing else. The pour and the set-down are the only sounds.

- **7-second teaser:** beat 1's first line over beat 3's swap.

**BINDING CLAIMS NOTE:** the underlying source is **Travison et al., *Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism*, 2007**, *"A Population-Level Decline in Serum Testosterone Levels in American Men"*, using the **Massachusetts Male Aging Study** across waves in **1987-89, 1995-97 and 2002-04**, reporting an **age-matched decline of roughly 1.2% per year** that persisted after controlling for chronic illness, general health, medications, smoking, BMI, employment and marital status. **Peer-reviewed.** **The script deliberately names no study and no person on camera**, so nothing here depends on a source the viewer cannot check in one search. **Verify the figures before shooting.**

## SCRIPT 2: "Find The Word" (~65s)

**Freshness check:** New subject, never scripted. **Deliberately distinct from "Read It Out Loud" (18 September), which reads a clause aloud from paper with no commentary: this is a SEARCH, the drama is a hit count next to a search box, and the presenter talks throughout.** Also distinct from today's Deep Production evergreen, which is a wordless envelope demonstration.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** A company in the memory-keeping category filed Chapter 11 in May 2024 and then came out the other side because an investor bought the assets. It is operating and expanding today.

**Format:** screen recording of a laptop. A terms-of-service page open. The presenter uses the find function on camera.

| # | [SHOT] | VO / on-screen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [SHOT: a terms page on screen, scrolled to the top.] | **VO:** "Everybody worries that a company holding their stuff will shut down. Here is the outcome nobody plans for." |
| 2 | [SHOT: keyboard shortcut, search box appears, types **assign**.] | **[ON SCREEN: ctrl + F]** **VO:** "Open the terms of any service you have given something to. Search for the word assign." |
| 3 | [SHOT: hit counter visible.] | **VO:** "That clause is usually there, and it usually says the same thing: the agreement can be transferred if the company is acquired, merged or sold." |
| 4 | [SHOT: types **merger**, then **bankruptcy**.] | **VO:** "Try merger. Try bankruptcy. Most consumer terms allow your account to move to whoever ends up owning the company." |
| 5 | [SHOT: cursor rests on the clause.] | **VO:** "So the question is not only what happens if they stop. It is who is allowed to take over, and whether what they promised you travels with your files." |
| 6 | [SHOT: presenter to camera, first face shot.] | **VO:** "Earlier this year a company in exactly our category went through Chapter 11 and came out the other side, because an investor bought the assets. It is running in museums now. That is a good outcome for the company." |
| 7 | [SHOT: hold.] | **VO:** "It also means the people who signed up with a small team that cared about their recordings are now customers of an investment firm they have never heard of. Nothing was broken. Everything moved." |
| 8 | [SHOT: hold, plain.] | **VO:** "We use a third-party accredited facility, which is a real answer to what happens if we stop, and only half an answer to who could end up holding this. A third party can be sold too. I would rather tell you that than let you find out." |
| 9 | [SHOT: close.] | **[ON SCREEN: SEARCH THE WORD BEFORE YOU SEAL ANYTHING.]** **VO:** "Search the word. Before you give anything to anybody, including us." |

**Platform cuts:**
