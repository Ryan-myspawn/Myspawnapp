## SCRIPT 2: "Whose Side Is This Number On?" (~65s)

**Freshness check:** **Sticky notes last appeared 23 August, thirty-three days out**, well clear of the window, and they have never carried a sorting action. **The SUBJECT is today's and it is new fleet-wide: sorting claims by who they flatter.** **Honest adjacency, flagged rather than buried:** the dating series is about *when* a number was measured; this is about *who benefits from it being believed*, which is a different question and a different test. **Two-column whiteboard ran 23 September and is excluded; this is a physical sort on a flat surface, not a drawn build.**
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55.
**Trend / hook used:** This afternoon's finding that a competitor's price sat wrong in our own published article for five weeks, **in their favor**, because nobody on our side had a reason to check it.

**Sound:** No music. The peel of each sticky note is the rhythm of the piece. Let them be loud.

| # | Shot | Line |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [SHOT: overhead, bare table. A pad of sticky notes and a marker. A hand writes one word on the first note: a number, no context.] | **VO:** "Here is a test that takes ninety seconds and I only learned it today by getting caught." |
| 2 | [SHOT: hand sticks the first note down on the left.] | **VO:** "Write down the numbers you repeat. Not all of them. The five you actually say out loud to people." |
| 3 | [SHOT: three more notes written and placed in a rough row.] | **VO:** "Now do not check whether they are true. Sort them by who they make look good." |
| 4 | [SHOT: hand drags two notes to the left side of the table, decisively.] | **[ON SCREEN: FLATTERS ME]** **VO:** "These ones flatter you. Somebody out there is already checking these, because being right about them costs your competition something." |
| 5 | [SHOT: hand drags two notes to the right.] | **[ON SCREEN: FLATTERS SOMEONE ELSE]** **VO:** "These ones flatter somebody else. Nobody is checking these. Not you, because they make you look worse. Not them, because they are winning." |
| 6 | [SHOT: hand taps the right-hand pile twice.] | **VO:** "Start here. This is where errors live longest." |
| 7 | [SHOT: eye level, presenter, plain, no lean-in.] | **VO:** "I know because we published a competitor's annual price for five weeks and it was wrong by about half, in their favor. Our own notes contradicted it twice. Nobody noticed, and the reason nobody noticed is that it made us look worse." |
| 8 | [SHOT: hold.] | **VO:** "I still cannot tell you the right number, so I am not going to guess at one on camera. That is a separate problem and it is the one I would rather have." |
| 9 | [SHOT: close on the right-hand pile, alone in frame.] | **[ON SCREEN: myspawn.me]** **VO:** "Check the ones that make you look worse. Everyone else is busy checking the ones that make you look better." |

**Platform cuts.**
- **TikTok (65s, full):** as written. The sticky-note peel at beats 4 and 5 is the loop point.
- **Reels (52s):** merge 1 and 2. **Beat 7 is the proof and cannot be cut**: without a real example this is a productivity aphorism.
- **Shorts (58s):** open on beat 9, the line, then reset to beat 1. It is the most quotable sentence in the batch.
- **7-second teaser:** [SHOT: two piles of notes.] **VO:** "Sort your facts by who they flatter." [Hand taps the right pile.] "Then check that one."

**Risk flags.**
1. **No company is named, and beat 7 must stay at "a competitor's annual price".** **We have not read their pricing page**, the currency is unestablished, and naming them while admitting we cannot verify the figure would be worse than saying nothing.
2. **Beat 8 is not optional.** Without it, the piece implies we have since found the right number. We have not.
3. **Keep beat 6 flat.** "This is where errors live longest" is an observation, not a gotcha.

**Captions.**
- **TikTok A:** "Sort the numbers you repeat by who they flatter. Check the ones that flatter somebody else first, because nobody else is going to."
- **TikTok B:** "We published a competitor's price wrong by half, in their favor, for five weeks. Our own notes contradicted it twice."
- **Reels A:** "Errors live longest where nobody has a reason to look."
- **Reels B:** "A number that makes you look bad gets checked by you. A number that makes your rival look good gets checked by nobody."
- **Shorts A:** "Ninety seconds, a pad of sticky notes, and one uncomfortable pile."
- **Shorts B:** "I still do not know the right number, and I am not guessing at one on camera."
- **Hashtags:** #business #research #medialiteracy #startup

## STATIC AD: Ad_SurvivingIsNotImproving_156

**Style:** emotional-demographic, single object, copy-led. **Theme:** today's Deep Production evergreen, which is the only non-measurement idea the fleet produced in 48 hours.

- **Headline:** **Surviving is not the same as improving.**
- **Subhead:** Almost everything you plan to pass on is in the first category.
- **Body (feed copy, under the creative):** A cast-iron pan gets better every time it is used. Almost nothing else does. Your files do not. Your phone does not. The photographs in the drawer are on a slow clock and so is the hard drive they were copied to. Most of what we mean by "keeping something" is really just slowing down the rate it degrades, and that is worth knowing before you decide what deserves a copy and what deserves a plan. **MySpawn stores the biological record at $99 a year, held by an independently accredited third-party facility. Storage only, no result, no score. First 1,000 members lock the founding rate.**
- **Lockup:** myspawn.me
- **Placements:** paid, square and vertical.

**Unsplash brief for the image agent.** Search **"cast iron pan"**, **"worn tool handle"**, **"well used skillet"**, **"seasoned cast iron"**.
**Requirements:** **no identifiable faces and no person in frame**; **no readable brand marks** (cast iron is frequently stamped with a maker's name on the handle or base: **check the handle at full resolution before use**); **no food in frame**, which would read as a recipe ad.
**Anti-repetition constraints, checked against the log:** the object family is **new to the ad lane**. **Excluded as saturated: draped textile (152, 23 Sep), architecture (148 and 151), documents and leather (150), seating (four kills in five days), botanical (154, yesterday), luggage (149, yesterday), scientific instrument (155, yesterday).**
**All three standing description rules apply:** read the **full description**, not the thumbnail; read for **provenance**, because a perfectly lit pan on a white sweep is very likely a cookware brand's product shoot; read for **medium**, because 3D renders are indexed as photographs.
**Fallback subject if nothing clears:** **a worn wooden tool handle** or **a leather belt creased from use**: same idea, different object, both new to the lane.
