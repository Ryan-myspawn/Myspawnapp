## SCRIPT — "Eight Trillion Dollars Is Not a Result" (~75s)

**Freshness check:** New. Today's AM script uses the conference as a *place* (the access gap); this
uses a *specific claim made about the sector* and is a different argument entirely. Distinct from
longpost #64 and the Sept 12 explainer, which sorted **company funding** into buckets — this is about
**market-size projections**, which are a different species of number and one we have never addressed.
Device is new: a restaurant bill folder. The 7-day run is stamp, pill organiser, whiteboard, phone
contacts, cloud-drive screen record, stopwatch, kitchen scale, mirror, combination lock,
comment-reply, two-chair, zero-prop walk, index cards, document-read.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55** (29-year-old optimizer lead; he is the one being sold the category).
**Trend-hook used:** the Longevity Investors Conference, **Sept 14-17, 2026, Le Grand Bellevue,
Gstaad** — seventh edition, running now — and the numbers attached to its 2026 announcement.

[SHOT 1: a leather bill folder closed on a table. He opens it. It is empty.]
VO: "There's a longevity investors conference running in Switzerland right now. Its co-founder, Tobias Reichmuth, said this about the sector."

[SHOT 2: to camera, reading it straight, no inflection.]
VO: "'The longevity sector is rapidly emerging as one of the most compelling investment themes of our time, with projections valuing it at over six hundred billion dollars by 2026.' And alongside it, a projection of eight trillion by 2030. Bigger than AI."
ON-SCREEN: "$600B by 2026 · $8T by 2030 — projections, quoted"

[SHOT 3: he taps the empty folder.]
VO: "I want to be fair to the man. He's a conference founder quoting market projections, which is a completely normal thing for a conference founder to do, and the science on that stage is real. Horvath, Barzilai, de Grey — those are serious names."

[SHOT 4: flat, holding the folder open.]
VO: "But here's what a market-size number actually measures. It measures what people are expected to spend. Not what works."

[SHOT 5: beat.]
VO: "You can have an eight-trillion-dollar market with zero approved therapies. In fact that's what a market that size mostly is: supplements, tests, clinics, subscriptions. Spending is the thing being forecast. Results are not in the number."

[SHOT 6: he closes the folder.]
VO: "So when you see that figure — and you will, because it's a very good figure — run the three buckets. Published result. Running, no result. Money. A market projection isn't even money yet. It's a forecast of money. It's the third bucket, one step further out."

[SHOT 7: to camera, plain.]
VO: "None of that makes the field a scam. It makes it early. And the only honest thing you can buy in an early field is the boring part that's already finished. Ninety-nine a year, and the record's done the day you do it."
