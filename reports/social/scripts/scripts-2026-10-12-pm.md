## SCRIPT 1: "The Model and the Thing"

**Freshness check:** this angle has not been used in the last 7 days of scripts files. **`blastoid`, `embryo model` and `Cell Systems` return ZERO across every scripts file and every production asset.** **DISCLOSED CASCADE: the 15:06 pack contains a 6-to-10-minute explainer on blastoids. This is 50 seconds and carries ONE idea from it. The explainer teaches what a blastoid is; this teaches what "looks like" is worth.** **Hook structure new: an object-swap reveal, which no script in the last seven days uses.**
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55 (29-year-old optimizer leads).
**Trend-hook used:** a ***Cell Systems*** blastoid study, covered **7 October 2026**. [1]
**Length:** 50 seconds.

**THE NUMBERS ARE NOT IN THIS SCRIPT, AND THAT IS A DECISION RATHER THAN AN OVERSIGHT.** The radar's own condition was **"re-source from *Cell Systems* before shooting, or cut the numbers and keep the lesson."** **A fetch was attempted at 17:2x and failed on DNS.** **So the numbers are cut.** **This is the second time today this operation has removed material it could not verify rather than shipping it with a verify-first note attached, and the reason is the same: a note is a hope that somebody downstream reads it.**

### Script

**[SHOT 1: two identical-looking objects side by side on a dark surface. A real apple and a wax apple. Close, shallow depth. 4 sec]**
**VO:** "One of these is an apple."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "One of these is an apple."]**

**[SHOT 2: a thumb presses into the left one. It gives. 3 sec]**
**VO:** "You found out by touching it. That took one second and it cost you nothing."

**[SHOT 3: cut to a petri dish under a lamp, a small pale cluster. No lab coat, no face. 5 sec]**
**VO:** "Scientists build things called blastoids. Stem cells, arranged so they organise themselves into the shape of a very early embryo."

**[SHOT 4: same dish, held still. 6 sec]**
**VO:** "They are built to be studied, because studying real human embryos is restricted, limited and ethically fraught. That is a good reason and it is the honest case for them."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "A model is built to be useful. Not identical."]**

**[SHOT 5: back to the two apples. 6 sec]**
**VO:** "Here is the part that matters. A study covered five days ago compared four different ways of building them against real embryo cells. And instead of reporting how close they got, it reported how far apart the four methods were from each other."

**[SHOT 6: the wax apple alone in frame. 7 sec]**
**VO:** "Which means blastoid is not one thing with one fidelity. Some recipes are close. At least one is not close at all. And all of them get described with the same word."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Same word. Different distances."]**

**[SHOT 7: direct, flat, no music. 7 sec]**
**VO:** "So when you read that scientists grew something in a lab, the question is not whether they did. It is which recipe, and how far from the real thing the best measurement puts it."

**[SHOT 8: black card. 5 sec]**
**VO:** "These are models of embryos. Not embryos. And nothing to do with lab-grown eggs or sperm, which is a different problem with a different wall in front of it."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "myspawn.me · we are not in this research"]**

**SOUND/MUSIC:** a single low tone under shots 1 to 2, nothing after. **The apple press has to be audible, so the room tone stays up.**

### Platform cut notes

- **Reels:** as written. The two-object open reads cleanly in the Reels crop.
- **Shorts:** as written, and put the scope line from SHOT 8 in the pinned comment as well, because Shorts viewers skip the last card.

## SCRIPT 2: "They Cost Twice What We Do"

**Freshness check:** this angle has not been used, and **`Acorn` returns ZERO across every scripts file.** **NEW SHAPE FOR THIS CHANNEL: a price comparison that spends its second half explaining why the comparison is unfair.** No script in the last seven days names a competitor's price at all.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55 (50-year-old estate planner leads; the 38-year-old provider is secondary).
**Trend-hook used:** today's competitor dive, which established a competitor's published storage pricing for the first time in six attempts. [2]
**Length:** 55 seconds.
**Lane:** comparison, with the counterweight built in.

### Script

**[SHOT 1: a single printed page on a table, two lines of figures visible. No face. 4 sec]**
**VO:** "A competitor publishes their storage price. Most in this category do not, so this is worth looking at."

**[SHOT 2: close on the first figure. 5 sec]**
**VO:** "One hundred and ninety dollars a year. Or sixteen hundred and fifty for ten years, which works out at about a hundred and sixty-five."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "$190/yr"]**

**[SHOT 3: a second page slides in beside it. 4 sec]**
**VO:** "Ours is ninety-nine dollars a year, billed annually. Roughly half."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "$99/yr"]**

**[SHOT 4: a hand covers the figures. 6 sec]**
**VO:** "Now I am going to take the comparison away from you, because it is not a fair one and I would rather say so than let you find out."

**[SHOT 5: direct, flat. 8 sec]**
**VO:** "They are storing living cells. Cryogenically. Cells that are supposed to still be alive when somebody thaws them. That requires a machine that cannot fail for decades."

**[SHOT 6: a still frame of a dry, sealed container. No frost, no vapour. 7 sec]**
**VO:** "We are storing a DNA record. Dry. At room temperature. There is no compressor in our model because there is nothing in our model that has to stay alive."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "Living cells. Or a readable record. Different jobs."]**

**[SHOT 7: both pages again, side by side. 7 sec]**
**VO:** "Cryo costs more because it is harder, not because anybody is overcharging. If you need living cells, pay the two hundred. We cannot do that job and I am not going to pretend we can."

**[SHOT 8: black card. 6 sec]**
**VO:** "The honest version of our pitch is narrow. If what you want preserved is the information, we are half the price and we have one fewer thing that can break."

**[ON SCREEN TEXT: "myspawn.me · $99/year, billed annually · storage only"]**

**SOUND/MUSIC:** none at all. **Paper and voice. A price comparison with a music bed sounds like an ad for the price; this one is an argument against its own headline and the silence carries that.**

### Platform cut notes
- **TikTok:** lead with SHOT 4, the hand covering the figures, and the line "I am going to take the comparison away from you." **The reversal is the hook; the prices can come second.**
- **Reels:** as written. Reels tolerates the slower build to the reversal.
- **Shorts:** as written.
- **7-SECOND TEASER:** SHOT 2 and SHOT 3, the two figures only, ending on "$99/yr". **Text card: "And here is why that is not the point."** The teaser deliberately sets up a claim the full cut dismantles.
- **PLACEMENT NOTE: organic first.** **A paid placement of a competitor price comparison invites a response we have no budget to have.** Organic, where the second half is visible, is where this is honest.
