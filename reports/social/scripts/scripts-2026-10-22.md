### Script

**[SHOT 1 - static wide, three identical wooden chairs in a row against a plain wall, nobody in frame, hold two full seconds before the first word]**
VO: "Nobody is sitting in these yet. That is the whole problem with inheritance."
ON SCREEN: THE THIRD CHAIR

**[SHOT 2 - slow push to chair one, framed off-centre]**
VO: "Chair one decided. Somebody in that chair chose what happens to everything they built."

**[SHOT 3 - the push continues, passing chair two without stopping]**
VO: "Chair two was supposed to be the one it went to. Often it is not."

**[SHOT 4 - settle on chair three]**
VO: "Chair three is you. And by the time it reaches you, the decision is decades old and the person who made it is not available for questions."

**[SHOT 5 - cut back to the static wide, all three chairs, no movement at all]**
VO: "Here is the part that gets skipped. In a survey of six hundred high-net-worth people across Asia-Pacific, Europe and the United States, sixty-five percent of the Gen X and millennial heirs said they had regrets about what they did with the money. Nearly two in five wished they had invested more of it."
ON SCREEN CARD, held for the whole of shot 5: HIGH-NET-WORTH RESPONDENTS ONLY / NOT A US-ONLY SAMPLE / SURVEYED FEB TO MAR, PUBLISHED JUNE 2025

**[SHOT 6 - same frame, no cut, presenter still absent]**
VO: "Read that back. The money arrived, and most of them wished they had done something else with it."

**[SHOT 7 - presenter walks into frame from the right, sits in chair three, looks along the row at the two empty chairs rather than at the lens]**
VO: "So if you are the one in chair one, here is a question worth more than the money. What are you leaving that cannot be spent wrong?"

**[SHOT 8 - rack focus off the presenter and onto the two empty chairs. Hold. Cut on the last syllable, no fade]**
