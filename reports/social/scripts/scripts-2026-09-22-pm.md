## DELTA SWEEP

### The catch: a June round inside a September roundup

A longevity roundup published as **September 2026 funding news** reports **$613.9 million in disclosed longevity funding**, of which **NewLimit represents 71 percent.** Seventy-one percent of $613.9M is about **$436 million**, which is **NewLimit's $435M Series C**, and that round closed on **2 June 2026**, led by Founders Fund with Thrive Capital, Lilly Ventures, Nat Friedman and Daniel Gross, at a valuation of about **$3.1 billion.**

**All of which is already on our file**, logged when the correction was made on 18 September after an earlier draft misstated it as a $130M Series B.

**So the item is not news. What it is, is a demonstration.** A reader scanning a piece headed *September funding news* takes away that longevity raised $613.9M this month and that one company took most of it. **The largest component is nearly four months old.** The roundup is probably cumulative and probably not trying to mislead anyone. **It does not have to be trying. The reader still walks away with a September number that is mostly June.**

**Primary audience:** Men 25-55.

**BINDING PRE-SHOOT CHECKS.**
