Gov. Newsom signed a 13-bill package including "Adam's Law" and AB 1709: companion chatbots must
carry crisis protocols for suicidal ideation, parental controls, age verification, and notification
if a child disables safety settings; social platforms may not serve behaviourally addictive features
to under-16s; toys containing AI companion chatbots are banned.
*Epistemic label: official government announcement (gov.ca.gov, Sept 10 2026) plus mainstream
reporting (NBC News, EdSource, office of Sen. Padilla). Statute text not read by me — the specific
provisions above are as reported, and the script does not quote the bill.*

**2. MIT Technology Review published Innovators Under 35 today.** (USABLE — second script)
Nine biotech honorees, selected from 550 nominations by 44 judges. The headline entries: a cellular
*reprogramming* therapy (Yuancheng "Ryan" Lu, 34) that reversed vision loss in aged blind mice in a
2020 study, with a near-identical therapy now being tested in people with eye disease; tiny brain
electrodes; and a personalised gene-editing treatment made for a single baby with a rare disorder.
*Epistemic labels, kept separate on purpose: the list is an **editorial selection**, not peer review.
The 2020 mouse work is **peer-reviewed**. The human eye trial is **in progress with no published

## SCRIPT 1 — "One Chair Has a Law" (~70s)

**Freshness check:** The 17:00 package argued the *ethics* of griefbots (Script 1, "The Word for What
We Don't Do"). This argues something that did not exist until yesterday: the *regulatory* line, and
where it stops. Different claim, different evidence, different ending. Device is new — the 7-day run
is entirely prop-driven (whiteboard, glovebox, kit box, letter cards, tape measure, index cards,
petri dishes, typing screen, printed page); this is staging, two chairs, nothing held.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55.**
**Trend-hook used:** California's companion-chatbot laws, signed Sept 10 2026 — under 48 hours old.

[SHOT 1: two plain chairs facing camera, side by side. One occupied, one empty. He sits in the left one.]
VO: "Yesterday California wrote the first real rulebook for AI companions. Crisis protocols. Age checks. A parent gets told if a kid switches the safety off. It's named after a teenager, and I'm going to leave it at that."

[SHOT 2: he pats the arm of his own chair, once.]
VO: "Every one of those protections is for a person who is alive. That's the right place to start. Nobody's arguing."

[SHOT 3: he looks at the empty chair. Holds it. Doesn't speak for a beat.]
VO: "There is no law for that one."
ON-SCREEN: "no crisis protocol · no age check · no consent"

[SHOT 4: back to camera, flat.]
VO: "If a company builds a chatbot of your father, nothing signed yesterday applies. He can't be screened for risk. He can't disable a setting. He cannot be notified. He has no standing at all, and the person paying the subscription isn't him."

[SHOT 5: plain.]
VO: "I'm not asking for a law. I'm pointing out that the market for the dead is the one part of this that is still entirely on the honour system — and I'm in that market."

[SHOT 6: he stands. The camera stays on both chairs, one now empty, one still empty.]
VO: "So here's ours, on the record. We store DNA records. We store video and voice a member recorded themselves, sealed until a date they chose. We don't generate anyone's voice. We don't build a model of a person. Ninety-nine a year, storage only. Hold us to it while nobody's making us."

SOUND NOTE: room tone only. The beat in SHOT 3 is the whole video — do not score it, do not cut it short. No music at any point.
**Platform cuts:** TikTok full 70s; Reels same; Shorts open on SHOT 3 (the empty chair + "There is no law for that one"). 7-second teaser: SHOT 1 first line + the SHOT 3 look.

**CAPTIONS:**
- TikTok v1: "California just regulated AI companions. It only covers the living."
- TikTok v2: "There's now a rulebook for chatbots of the living. There is none for the dead."
- Reels v1: "The new chatbot laws are good. Here's the gap nobody's discussing."
- Reels v2: "I run a preservation company. The market I'm in has no rules at all."
- Shorts v1: "One chair has a law. The other doesn't."
- Shorts v2: "Hold us to it while nobody's making us."
- Hashtags: #ai #policy #legacy #ethics (trim per platform)

**Footnotes:** Newsom signed the package Sept 10 2026 (gov.ca.gov; NBC News; EdSource; Sen. Padilla's
office). Provisions are as reported; the script quotes no statutory text.
**RAILS (hard):** (a) the law is named for a real teenager who died — name it once as "named after a
teenager" and move on; **never** use the name, the circumstances, or any detail as a hook, and never

VO: "MIT Technology Review put out its under-35 list this morning. Nine of them work in biology. I read all nine, and I want to be straight about where my own company sits on that page."

[SHOT 2: still walking.]
VO: "One of them reversed vision loss in old blind mice by partially resetting their cells — that was published in 2020, and a near-identical therapy is in people right now for eye disease. Not finished. Running. No results yet, and anyone telling you otherwise is guessing."

[SHOT 3: walking.]
VO: "Another one built a gene-editing treatment for exactly one baby. Not a drug for a category. A drug for a person."

[SHOT 4: he stops walking. First time the frame is still.]
VO: "Every headline on that list is about changing biology. Not one of them is about keeping a record of it. Including mine — I'm not on that list and I'm not going to be, because what I do isn't clever."

[SHOT 5: to camera, plain.]
VO: "But notice what the one-baby treatment needed before anything else happened: somebody had to read that specific kid's genome. You can't personalise medicine to a person you never sequenced."

[SHOT 6: beat. No push.]
VO: "That is not me promising you a cure. Storing your DNA with me does not get you treated, and I'll say that as many times as it takes. It means the record exists. Ninety-nine a year. The clever people are doing the hard part — I'm just making sure there's something of you left to be clever about."
