## THREADS POSTS (2)

**T-A (the quietly interesting fact)**
> Something I did not expect to find in a federal regulation this week.
>
> If a US clinical lab shuts down, the rules require it to make provisions so that records, slides, blocks and tissue are still retained for the full required period. Somebody wrote down what happens when the lab stops existing.
>
> The consumer DNA industry has no equivalent. What happens there is whatever the contract says, interpreted by a bankruptcy process, at the moment when nobody is paying attention.
>
> The gap isn't secret. It just isn't on the box.

**T-B (the builder observation)**
> Two pieces of research this week ended up at the same place by accident.

> I don't think the fix is more output. I think it's naming three moments and building one thing for each.
