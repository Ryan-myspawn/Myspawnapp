## COMMENT-OPS KIT (pre-written honest replies for the ambient-storage conversation)

*For use under the explainer, the unboxing, and Ad_PaperProof when they post. House voice: concede first, source second, never defensive.*

1. **"Why isn't it frozen? Frozen is better."**
Reply: "Fair instinct, and for living cells you're right: eggs, sperm, embryos need cryo, full stop. For a DNA record, the data goes the other way: decades of room-temperature newborn-screening cards still yield readable genomes (PLOS One 2013, BMC Biotech 2014), and a 2025 head-to-head found frozen samples picked up MORE artifactual mutations from freeze-thaw. Dry and stable beats cold and cyclable for records. Sources in the video description."
2. **"What happens if you shut down?"**
Reply: "The only question that matters in this category. Samples are held by GenVault, an independent CAP-accredited, FDA-registered biorepository, not by our app, and the custody answers are written down, not vibes. We'd rather you ask this of every storage company, including us, before buying anything."
3. **"Is $99 forever? What's the catch?"**
Reply: "$99 per year, billed annually. The first 1,000 members lock that founding rate. No entry fee. If a preservation company hides the annual line, that's the catch; ours is the headline."
4. **"What would anyone even do with it?"**
Reply: "Honestly: we don't promise anyone anything, and we put 'storage only' in our own ads. The claim is narrower: sequencing tools keep improving and re-reading old samples (the newborn-card studies are literally that), and a record that exists has options a missing one never will. That's the whole pitch."
5. **"This is dystopian / selling immortality."**
Reply: "We'd agree if it were. No revival promises, no gene editing, no baby-making, no AI resurrection. It's a file cabinet for biology with an audit trail, priced like a phone case. The boring version is the honest version."
