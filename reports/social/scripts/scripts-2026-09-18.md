## PODCAST SEGMENT ONE-PAGER (Reaction Fuel item)

**Note:** today's Deep Production episode slot is committed to the TikTok subject. **This is a standalone segment, droppable into any episode**, and it does not duplicate Script 2: Script 2 describes the trial, this segment attacks the number.

**Cold open line:**
> "Somebody is going to tell you we are two to five years away from an FDA-approved age-reversal therapy. I want to show you how to check that in about ninety seconds, using nothing but a calendar."

**The claim being reacted to, stated honestly as unattributed:**
> *"Leading researchers think we're 2-5 years away from first FDA-approved epigenetic reprogramming therapies."*

**Provenance, and this matters:** the line circulates in longevity community content and newsletters. **No named researcher, no citation, and our sweep could not find an origin.** **On air, say that.** Do not attribute it to a person, a company or a publication, and do not imply bad faith: most of the people repeating it are enthusiasts who read it somewhere.

**Talking points with receipts:**
1. The first partial-reprogramming therapy entered a human on **9 June 2026** (Life Biosciences, ER-100). That is the clock's actual start.
2. It is a **Phase 1**, which asks about safety and tolerability, not effectiveness.
3. It is in **one organ**, the eye, chosen because it is contained.
4. **No results have been published.** Not weak results. None.
5. Phase 1 to approval for a gene therapy runs through Phase 2 and Phase 3 with enrollment, follow-up and review at each step. **A trial that dosed its first patient in June 2026 does not become an approved therapy in 2028 under any path that exists.**
6. The steelman, which must be said: the underlying science is real and the mouse work is genuinely striking. **The objection is to the number, not to the field.**
7. The general skill: when you hear "X years away," ask what has actually started, what phase it is in, and whether anything has been published. **Three questions, ninety seconds.**

**Clip-able 30-second close:**
> "I am not telling you this field is fake. I think it is one of the most interesting things happening in biology. I am telling you that the first human dose was in June, in one eye, in a safety study, with nothing published. Anyone who turns that into a delivery date is selling you a feeling. The science does not need the exaggeration, and neither do you."

**Shorts pull-quote moments:**
- "That is the clock's actual start: June ninth."
- "Not weak results. None."
- "Anyone who turns that into a delivery date is selling you a feeling."

**Right-of-publicity:** no individual is named, quoted or depicted. The claim is handled as an unattributed circulating line, which is what it is.
