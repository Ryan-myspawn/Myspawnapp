**Runtime:** ~70 seconds. **Platform:** Shorts and Reels primary, TikTok secondary.

**FORMAT: HE TURNS A WALL CALENDAR BACKWARD WHILE TALKING.** One locked shot on the calendar and his hands. **He starts on October and flips back one month at a time to May, and the number of pages is the argument.** **He is never in frame above the shoulder.**

**BINDING STANCE RULE, and it governs every cut: nothing in this piece may suggest an IVF-conceived child is damaged.** **The subject is the gap between a research date and a news date. **Sound:** room tone. **The page turns are the only percussion.** No music.
