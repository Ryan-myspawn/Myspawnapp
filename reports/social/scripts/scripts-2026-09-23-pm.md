## DELTA SWEEP

### The one dated item: "AI-assisted" arrives on a supplement label

**23 September 2026.** A consumer-health company announced the US and UK launch of a range of **"AI-assisted" longevity bioactives**, following an initial launch in Dubai, distributed through its own consumer health platform and backed by a named investment partner.

**Epistemic labels, stated before anything else, because they are most of the story:** this is a **company announcement on a paid press-release wire.** It is **not journalism, not peer review, and not a trial result.** The announcement's own language is comparatively careful: it describes the formulations as developed through biotechnology research, scientific literature, biological pathway analysis, formulation science **and artificial intelligence**, with the stated goal of **"exploring more targeted approaches"** to healthy aging.

**That phrasing is the responsible version and I am not going to strawman it.** The interesting thing is not this company. **It is the arrival of "AI-assisted" as a phrase on a consumer supplement, because that phrase is about to be everywhere and almost nobody will ask the one useful question about it.**

**The question is not whether AI was used. It is AT WHICH STEP.**

"AI-assisted" can honestly describe: a literature search. A pathway analysis. A formulation optimizer. A model that ranked candidate compounds. **Every one of those is upstream of the only thing that determines whether a supplement works, which is what happened when people took it.** AI can make a hypothesis faster and cheaper. **It cannot make a hypothesis true, and it does not substitute for the trial that would.**

**So this sits beside the dating question rather than replacing it.** "How old is that number" asks **when**. This asks **at which step**. Both are questions about where a claim actually comes from.

**Company not named in any asset below.** The term is the subject; the company is an example of a phrase that is about to be used by hundreds of others. **Punch at the idea.**

**Nothing else moved.** No platform policy, no dated science, no cultural moment.
