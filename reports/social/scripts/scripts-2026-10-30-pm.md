## THREADS POSTS

**THREADS 1.** Something I keep running into: two sound surveys can point opposite ways and neither is wrong, because they asked different questions. "What is the ideal family size" and "how many do you expect to have" are not the same question, and treating them as the same is how a data story turns into a panic. **If you only ever see one number quoted, that is a choice somebody made.**

**THREADS 2.** The useful question when the forecast is genuinely unknown is not "which future is coming." It is "which decisions cost me the same in every future." Those are the ones you can make now without needing to be right. **Very few decisions qualify. Keeping a record of something is one of them. MySpawn is $99 a year, billed annually, and it does not require anyone to predict anything.**
