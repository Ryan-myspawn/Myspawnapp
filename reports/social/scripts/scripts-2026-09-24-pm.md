## TWO THREADS POSTS

**1.**
> Here is a thing almost nobody knows about hair.
>
> A hair is not a growing thing. It is a column of cells that were killed on purpose. As they get pushed up out of the follicle they fill with keratin and then destroy their own nucleus, and what is left becomes the strand.
>
> That is why the old rule was "the root has the full profile, the shaft does not." Pull, do not trim.
>
> In 2026 a peer-reviewed paper reported that sequencing built for tiny broken fragments can read nuclear DNA out of a rootless shaft. The DNA was in there the whole time. The method could not read it.
>
> Which does not make a hairbrush a backup. It is a research technique, and a brush is hair from several people of unknown age handled by everyone in the house.
>
> But it is a good illustration of the only thing we actually sell: the sample has to survive long enough for the reader to get better.

**2.**
> A quiet request, and it costs nothing.
>
> Next time somebody sends you a big number about money, health or technology, do not argue with it. Just ask when it was measured.
>
> Not when the post went up. Not when you saw it. When someone actually counted the thing.
>
