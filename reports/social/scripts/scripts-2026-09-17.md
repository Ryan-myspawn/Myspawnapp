## STATIC AD DIRECTION: Ad_WhatIsWrittenOnTheBack

**Freshness check:** New claim and new visual. The log has no ad about photographs, identification or archives, and no ad has used a handwriting visual. Distinct from yesterday's Ad_TheOnlyNumbersRequired direction, which was a regulatory receipt.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55**, father/provider lead with a strong 40-60 read.
**Trend / hook used:** the Ancestor Photo Prank peaking this week.

**Headline:** WHAT IS WRITTEN ON THE BACK IS THE RECORD.

**Body:** A photograph with no date, no place and no names is not evidence of anything. Neither is a file called IMG_0042. Twenty seconds of handwriting outlives the paper it is on. Storage only, $99/yr.

**Visual direction:** the **reverse side of an old photograph**, shot flat and slightly raking so the handwriting catches the light. Pencil or fountain pen, genuinely aged paper, no printed text, **no visible names that identify a real living person** (illegible or partially obscured handwriting is preferred and is more evocative anyway). No faces, because the point is the side without one. Unsplash, real photography, per the standing photo mandate. **Kill any frame where a full name, address or date of birth is legible.**

**Why this ad:** it is the only asset in the batch that works with no headline knowledge and no trend context. Someone who has never heard of the prank still understands it.
