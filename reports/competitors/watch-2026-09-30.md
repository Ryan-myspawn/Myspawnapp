## FINDING 1: THE FLAGGED UNCERTAINTY IS RESOLVED, AND OUR PUBLISHED RANGE DID NOT CONTAIN ONE OF THE THREE PROVIDERS IT NAMED

**Yesterday this file flagged, but did not correct, a possibility: that our published up-front cord-blood range might be anchored on promotional pricing.** The rotation dive was given one job, to settle it. **It is settled and the problem is larger than the one flagged.**

**What `dna-banking-cost.md` printed**, verified live on 30 August 2026: **$710 to $1,195 up front plus $185 to $199 a year**, attributed to **CBR, Cryo-Cell and Cells4Life.**

**What today's CBR dive returns** ([cordblood.com enrollment and prepaid pages](https://www.cordblood.com/enroll), via search index; **the site was not opened**): annual storage at **$210 per sample per year**; a widely promoted option at **$1,795 up front then $400 a year**; prepaid plans at **$7,385 for eighteen years** with no yearly fees, and **$11,385 up front for lifetime storage**. Payment plans for initial fees reported from **$52 a month**, with referral savings and military pricing also offered.

**$210 and $400 are both above our $185 to $199 band. $1,795 is above our $710 to $1,195 band.** **Our range did not contain CBR on either axis.**

**A large headline discount percentage is advertised on the lifetime plan. It is deliberately NOT recorded here or in the article**, per the rule set on 28 September: record that a promotion exists and what it applies to, never the number.

**THE UNCOMFORTABLE PATTERN, AND IT IS THE THIRD IN A ROW.** Every correction this week has made a competitor look **more expensive** and therefore made us look better: SecuriGene on 26 September, the positive-control overstatement yesterday, and this today. **Three consecutive corrections in our own favor is exactly what the sort-by-who-they-flatter audit predicts of an archive whose unflattering numbers nobody has a motive to check.** **It is not evidence that we are lucky. It is a reason to go looking for the errors pointing the other way**, and the remaining unchecked prices in this file should be assumed to skew the same direction.

**ACTION TAKEN, and it follows the SecuriGene precedent exactly rather than inventing a new one.** `dna-banking-cost.md` has been **widened and flagged in four places including the JSON-LD**, to "roughly $710 to $1,795 up front plus roughly $185 to $400 a year, with prepaid multi-year and lifetime plans well above that, figures unverified", **with a dated correction note appended.** **The numbers were NOT substituted**, because swapping one unverified figure for another repeats the error instead of fixing it, and because today's figures are index-level too.
**The 30 August verification is not called dishonest in the note.** Prices move. **What was wrong was continuing to present a month-old range as current.**

## FINDING 2: CBR DISPLAYS AT LEAST FOUR PRICES FOR THE SAME SERVICE, WHICH SETTLES YESTERDAY'S QUESTION AT CATEGORY LEVEL

Reported alongside each other: **an annual fee, a promoted up-front-plus-annual option, an eighteen-year prepaid plan, and a lifetime prepaid plan.**

**Yesterday's finding was that a company has the price it shows you and the price it also has. Today's is that the ladder can have four rungs**, and the rung a customer meets depends on which page they land on and which promotion is running.

**SO WHAT FOR MYSPAWN, and it is the same positioning point sharpened by a second data point:** **we have one number. $99 a year, billed annually, and the first thousand members lock the founding rate.** **There is no ladder, no prepaid tier, no lifetime option and no promotional percentage**, which is unusual enough in this category that it is worth saying out loud and was invisible to us until we went and counted somebody else's rungs.
**THE RAIL THAT GOES WITH IT: a four-rung ladder is not dishonest.** Different families want different things and prepaid plans are a legitimate product. **No MySpawn asset may imply otherwise.** The contrast is that we have nothing to explain, not that they are hiding something.
