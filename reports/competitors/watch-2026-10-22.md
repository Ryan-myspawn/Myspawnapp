## THE DELIBERATE DIVE: SECURIGENE, AND IT CLOSES A FOUNDER-QUEUE QUESTION

**THE QUESTION, as it has sat in the queue: what is our honest positioning against SecuriGene?** **It can now be answered, and the answer is that the two products are not the same category and the price comparison is not like-for-like.**

**What today's dive confirmed from the company's own pages, read through a search index:**
- **The funeral provider collects the sample**, using supplies inside the kit.
- **Collection must happen BEFORE cremation or embalming.**
- **The DNA is extracted, purified, preserved and RETURNED TO THE FAMILY inside the capsule.**
- The capsule is **316L surgical-grade stainless steel, triple-sealed, with a built-in humidity regulator**.
- The home-banking product takes **10 to 14 business days** to process. *(The post-mortem timeline may differ and was not stated.)*

**THE POSITIONING ANSWER, in one line: SecuriGene sells POSSESSION, at-need, as a one-off purchase of an object the family keeps. MySpawn sells CUSTODY, pre-need, as an annual fee at a third-party accredited biorepository.**

**AND THE HONEST CONSEQUENCE, which cuts against the easy comparison we have been making: $498 once for an object you hold is NOT comparable to $99 a year for somebody else holding it.** **Per the binding pricing rule, a comparison whose thesis depends on one-time versus recurring is false and must be reworked rather than patched.** **The honest frame is custody risk, not cost: if you hold it, you are the single point of failure and nobody is monitoring anything. If they hold it, you depend on an institution's continuity and your own ability to reach it.** **Both are real risks. Stating both is the only version of this comparison we can defend.**
