### The detail that matters more than the price

**CBS News reports that Herasight charges $50,000 for the report but gave one couple half off in exchange for agreeing to share information about their future kids.**

**Reported accurately and attributed: this is a news organisation's account of one transaction, not something we verified, and it is not presented as a general policy.**

**SO WHAT FOR MYSPAWN, and it is the strongest differentiation proof this file has produced in weeks.** A discount granted in exchange for ongoing data about a child who does not yet exist is **a price paid in someone else's information, by someone who cannot consent and does not exist yet.** **Our entire positioning is custody transparency, and this is the cleanest available contrast: we charge $99 a year, billed annually, the number is on the page, and nothing about a future person is part of the consideration.** **BINDING IF USED IN ANY ASSET: name no company, quote the CBS framing accurately, and attack the pricing MECHANISM rather than the firm or any family involved.**
