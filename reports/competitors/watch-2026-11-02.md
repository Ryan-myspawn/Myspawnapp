## THE FINDING: 248 versus 263, and why we are NOT changing our number

**On 31 October this lane corrected its own baseline in public.** Our file had recorded "the Cryonics Institute, founded 1976, has over 2,000 registrants, and 263 are already in cryopreservation." **The 263 is Alcor's patient count, not the Cryonics Institute's**, and we published the correction in longpost #180 rather than fixing it quietly. The published figures were **roughly 263 Alcor patients, roughly 278 at the Cryonics Institute, around 1,500 living Alcor members.**

**Today's sweep surfaced a German-language profile stating that Alcor reports 1,442 members and 248 people cryopreserved.** **The membership figure is consistent with what we published: we said "around 1,500" and 1,442 sits inside that. The patient count is not: 248 against our 263.**

**WE ARE NOT CORRECTING OUR FIGURE, AND THE REASONING IS THE POINT.** **The article is UNDATED in everything the search index returned, so it cannot be placed in time directly. But a cryopreservation patient count is a MONOTONIC COUNTER: patients are admitted and, per Alcor's own record, there are no transfers out and no losses recorded. A monotonic counter reading LOWER is therefore reading EARLIER.** **248 is almost certainly an older snapshot of the same series, not a competing claim about the same moment.**

**SO AN UNDATED SOURCE CAN SOMETIMES BE ORDERED EVEN WHEN IT CANNOT BE DATED, and that is a genuinely useful tool for this lane, which drowns in undated secondary coverage.** **NEW METHOD NOTE: where a figure is a monotonic counter, a lower value establishes that the source is earlier, which lets two undated sources be sequenced without dating either.**

**THE CAVEAT, STATED BECAUSE IT MATTERS.** **The monotonicity itself comes from the same family of community listings that gave us 263.** If that assumption is wrong, the inference collapses and the two numbers are simply unreconciled. **So this is logged as a well-founded inference and explicitly NOT as a verified fact, and our published 263 keeps its own "roughly" and its own sourcing caveat.**

**SO WHAT FOR MYSPAWN: differentiation proof, and the cheapest kind.** We published a correction against ourselves two days ago. Today we found a figure that could have been used to publish a second, louder correction, and the honest answer was that it is probably just an older reading. **Not correcting is also a form of accuracy, and a company that only ever revises in the direction of drama is not being careful, it is being dramatic.**
