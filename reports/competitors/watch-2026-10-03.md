## FINDING 2: THE ROTATION'S ASSIGNED FIRST JOB, DELIVERED

Yesterday's run promoted Tomorrow.Bio ahead of the queue and specified: **its first job is a one-paragraph description, not a price.** This file has carried the company on its monitored list since August with pricing, a founder letter and a funding line, **and no description of what the company actually does.**

**Tomorrow.Bio, in one paragraph.** Founded in **2019 in Berlin** by **Dr. Emil Kendziorra** and **Fernando Azevedo Pinheiro**, it sells human and pet cryopreservation. The operational core is not a facility but a **response capability**: **SST teams (Standby, Stabilization and Transportation)** staffed by doctors, EMTs and personnel trained for the procedure, currently based in **Berlin and Zurich**, dispatched in advance when a death is foreseeable. On **legal pronouncement of death** the team begins **chest compressions with a mechanical device, supplies oxygen, administers medication and starts cooling with ice and water**, then **replaces body fluids with a cryoprotectant solution** to limit ice-crystal formation. **Cryo-ambulances** carry the patient while cooling continues, to storage at **minus 196 degrees Celsius** held by its sister organisation, the **European Biostasis Foundation in Rafz, Switzerland**. As of May 2025 coverage it had preserved **20 people and 10 pets** and reported **over EUR 160 million in pre-contracted commitments**. ([Tech.eu, 26 May 2025](https://tech.eu/2025/05/26/not-just-science-fiction-tomorrow-bio-has-preserved-20-people-and10-pets-for-future-revival/), [Silicon Canals](https://siliconcanals.com/cryonics-lab-tomorrow-bio-bags-e5m/), [company explainer on standby teams](https://www.tomorrow.bio/post/what-do-standby-teams-do). **All via search index.** **Both figures are from May 2025 coverage and should be assumed out of date, which is the lesson of Finding 1 applied immediately.**)

**So what for MySpawn: this is differentiation proof, and it is structural rather than rhetorical.**

**Their entire product is a clock.** Everything of value happens in the hours after a legal pronouncement, and it requires a trained team to physically reach the person in time. That is why the company's money goes into ambulances, standby rosters and geographic coverage rather than into storage.

**Ours has no clock at all.** Collection happens **while the person is alive**, at home, in about seven minutes, from hair and nail clippings, posted to an accredited biorepository. **There is no window to miss, no team to dispatch, and no geography to cover.**

**That is the honest reason the two are not substitutes, and it is a better line than any price comparison.** **It also cuts against us where it should:** what we store is a record, and what they attempt is a person. **Those are different ambitions and our copy should never blur them.** **No comparison claim of any kind is approved for creative from this entry.** The description exists so that this file can finally say what the company is.
