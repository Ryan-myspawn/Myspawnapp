## BONUS DIVE: SecuriGene / DNA Memorial

**The rotation gave this dive a specific job, set in writing yesterday: establish what it sells, at what price, and whether the storage fee is recurring or one-time, because that is the comparison our own customers will make.** **All three are now answered, and the answer is the one we would least have chosen.**

**THE PREDICTION MADE BEFORE RESEARCHING, so it can be scored honestly.** Yesterday's refined rule was: **a company publishes the prices it controls and ranges the prices a third party sets.** Since DNA Memorial sells through funeral homes, the rule predicted published direct prices and unpublished funeral-home prices. **CONFIRMED on both halves.** Direct prices are published to the dollar; **funeral-home channel prices are not published anywhere we could see.** That is one failure of the crude form (Acorn), one refinement, and now one clean confirmation.

### What it sells, and the prices

- **DNA Banking Capsule, the standard at-home product: $389.** SecuriGene's own page describes storing DNA samples **indefinitely at home without expensive equipment or annual storage fees.**
- **Post-Mortem DNA Banking Capsule: $498 USD**, with a Certificate of Analysis giving DNA quantity and purity. **Name-and-date engraving $29 per capsule; custom engraving $49.**
- **A reseller listing shows a $498 to $1,218 range** where the higher tiers bundle genetic testing: **paternal ancestry at $617 total, a comprehensive health test at $1,218 total.** **That listing is roughly 600 days old and is labelled stale rather than quoted as current.**
- **Forensic tissue cryopreservation, a DIFFERENT product: from $3,200, with paid storage tiers including 10 years at $2,000.** **Do not let this number migrate into a DNA-capsule comparison.**
- **Channel: DNA Legacy by SecuriGene states it is offered through a network of over 1,000 funeral homes across North America**, with families able to buy a DNA Preservation Kit from a funeral home or online.
- **Turnaround: 10 to 14 business days from receipt of samples at their laboratory.**

*(Every figure above is via search index. No page opened at its own domain. The "indefinitely" and "no annual storage fees" claims are the VENDOR'S OWN and carry no independent verification in our reading.)*

### The finding, stated against our own interest

**This is the first direct competitor this file has found whose pricing model is the one we are forbidden to claim.** Our binding pricing fact says MySpawn is **$99 a year, billed annually**, and that we may never write "one-time" or "no annual fee". **SecuriGene can write both, because for its capsule product they appear to be true.**

**The arithmetic, which a prospective customer will do in about four seconds:**

- **$389 once versus $99 a year breaks even at roughly 3.9 years.**
- **At five years we cost $495 against their $389. At ten years, $990 against $389.**
- **Below four years we are cheaper. Above four, we are not, and no amount of framing changes that.**

**We are not going to spin this.** The honest counter is not about price at all, and stating it clearly is the only defensible response:

**THE REAL AXIS IS CUSTODY, AND IT CUTS BOTH WAYS.** Their $389 buys a capsule **the household keeps and stores itself**. Our $99 a year buys **custody at an independently accredited third-party biorepository**, GenVault in New Jersey, **whose accreditations are GenVault's and not ours.** **Their model has no company to outlive and no annual invoice, and it depends entirely on a family not losing, binning or forgetting a small object across decades. Ours removes that failure mode and substitutes a different one: it depends on a company and a facility continuing to exist and continuing to be paid.** **Those are genuinely different bets and an honest page would say so in those words.**

**SECOND HONEST POINT: "indefinitely" is doing enormous work in their copy and it is unverified.** We have spent two weeks establishing in our own content that ambient-temperature claims measured over 28 days cannot carry a decades promise. **That scepticism applies to us first and to them equally. We are not going to attack their claim using a standard we do not meet ourselves.**

### Tactic worth countering, and it is not the price

**The tactic is the FUNERAL HOME.** Over 1,000 North American funeral homes is a distribution network at the exact moment a family is thinking about what remains of a person, sold by someone already trusted in that room. **No amount of paid social reaches a customer in that moment.** **That, not the $389, is the structural advantage.** **We have no equivalent and no plausible route to one without the partnership lane the Prospector has been unable to progress for eight weeks.**
