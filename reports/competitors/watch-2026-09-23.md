## Deep dive #28: StoryWorth marketing snapshot

**Full entry appended to `baseline.md`.** The three tactics, in short:

1. **The decoy tier.** Basic appears to exist to anchor buyers toward Color, because black-and-white is a hard sell for a book of family photographs. Competent, not dishonest. **We have no tier ladder at all, which is a simplicity advantage to keep rather than a gap to fill.**
2. **The renewal that flatters the headline.** Unlimited is reported to auto-renew at **$99/yr against $199 upfront**, making year one read as promotional. **Counter, and it is ours by right: we say $99/year and mean $99/year, with no upfront that differs from the renewal.** **This is the clearest place our pricing is simply easier to explain than a competitor's, and we have never once said so in copy.**
3. **Headline versus outlay.** $150 to $230 actual, per independent write-ups.

**So what: differentiation proof, not pricing pressure.** StoryWorth sells **a finished object**, delivered and done. We sell **a held thing with a date on it.** The honest framing is complementary: **a StoryWorth book is a good thing to put in a Memory Vault and is not a substitute for one.**

**One threat to watch:** **if the reported $99 Color tier is real, the category incumbent's mid-tier sits exactly on our price**, and that would be the second independent legacy product to land on $99/year after Remento. **Verify before any copy is built on it.**
