## THE FINDING: the Nucleus whitepaper chase, and what it actually settled

**YESTERDAY THIS FILE RECORDED AN EXACT NEXT STEP: "read the Nucleus whitepaper itself, record the gain it states with its own units and date, and only then compare it to the 14-point marketing figure. If the whitepaper cannot be reached, record THAT as the finding rather than quoting the aggregator."**

**BOTH HALVES HAPPENED, AND THE ORDER MATTERS.**

**THE WHITEPAPER COULD NOT BE OPENED.** It exists at a known address on the company's own domain, `mynucleus.com/labs/traits-whitepaper`, and **`Traits Whitepaper` and `mynucleus` were both fleet-zero before today.** **Thirty-third checked day with no source opened at its own domain.** **Per the instruction, the unreachability IS the recorded finding.**

**BUT THE DAY IS NOT A FAILURE, BECAUSE THE SOURCING IMPROVED IN A SPECIFIC WAY.** Yesterday the 14-point claim rested on ONE low-authority aggregator. **Today a SECOND, independent carrier reports the whitepaper's own internal numbers.** That is not the primary source, and it is materially better than one aggregator repeating a headline.

**WHAT THE WHITEPAPER IS REPORTED TO SAY, all labelled as reported-from-secondary and none of it verified at source:**
- **Within-family accuracy: about 43 percent of height variance, 18 percent of BMI variance, and 19 percent of intelligence variance.**
- **An expected SPREAD between the highest-scoring and lowest-scoring of FIVE embryos of about 10.8 IQ points.** [^1]

**THE DISTINCTION THAT DOES ALL THE WORK, AND IT IS THE REASON THIS WAS WORTH CHASING: A SPREAD IS NOT A GAIN.** The spread is top minus bottom. **The gain is top against the AVERAGE embryo, which an independent analysis of the whitepaper puts at roughly half the spread, near 5 IQ points.** [^1]

**AND THE PEER-REVIEWED FLOOR, WHICH IS THE STRONGEST SOURCE IN THIS ENTRY AND IS ALREADY ON OUR FILE ELSEWHERE: Karavani et al., *Cell*, 2019, estimated an average gain from screening of about 2.5 IQ points with the scores available then.** [^2] **`Karavani` returns eight fleet files; it is not new to MySpawn, but it has never sat beside these other three numbers.**

**SO THE SHAPE, AND THE SHAPE IS NOW QUOTABLE WHERE THE HEADLINE NUMBER ALONE WAS NOT: a marketing figure around 14, a company-reported spread around 10.8, a derived gain around 5, and a peer-reviewed estimate of about 2.5.** **Every one of those is a model estimate rather than a measured outcome, including the peer-reviewed one.**

**DO-NOT-QUOTE STATUS: PARTIALLY LIFTED, WITH THREE BINDING CONDITIONS.**
1. **The 14-point figure may be referred to ONLY as a marketing claim, never as a finding, and never without the whitepaper-derived numbers beside it.**
2. **The 10.8 and the roughly 5 are SECONDARY REPORTS OF A DOCUMENT WE HAVE NOT READ and must carry that label every time.**
3. **The peer-reviewed 2.5 is the only number here with a journal and a year, and it is the one to lead with if only one is used.**
