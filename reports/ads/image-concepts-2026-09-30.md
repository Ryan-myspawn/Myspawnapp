## THE STRUCTURAL FINDING, AND IT IS ABOUT OUR OWN TEMPLATE RATHER THAN ABOUT UNSPLASH

**Our 1080x1080 layout gives the photograph only its top 40 percent.** Below that sits a 669px gradient and a 260px scrim, and everything in that band is decoration rather than subject.

**Therefore a photograph whose MEANING is a vertical sequence cannot be read in the square at all.** Today's shelf photograph carries three stacked shelves. **Every crop that showed more than one shelf pushed the rest under the gradient; every crop that kept the frame clean showed a single dark bar.** Four crops were tried. **The subject is countable tiers and the square can only ever show one of them.**

**The same photograph in 9x16 reads immediately**, because the vertical layout leaves roughly 60 percent of the frame clear and a stack has room to be a stack.

**RULE PROPOSED AND APPLIED TODAY: a subject whose meaning is a vertical sequence (shelves, stairs, a stack, a ladder of rungs) is briefed for 9x16 or not at all.** This also explains, retroactively, why the ladder photographs that did work (**Ad_BottomRung_73**, **Ad_SixteenPointsApart_159**) were **leaning diagonals**: a diagonal reads at any crop. **A vertical stack does not.**
