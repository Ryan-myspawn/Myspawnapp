## COMPLIANCE PASS
- **Storage only / no descendant promises:** PASS with one required guardrail — M1/T1/RSA-D3 use the embryo record strictly as *storage* proof and say "storage only" / "the future can figure out the rest." Never let an edit imply a stored DNA sample can become a child; the embryo was living tissue, our product is a record. Any variant that adds "someday science could use it to…" requires legal review first.
- **Accreditations:** attributed to the facility, not MySpawn ("stored with GenVault, an independent, accredited US biorepository"; ads shorthand "accredited US biorepository/lab" never says MySpawn holds ISO/CAP/FDA. In M1/M2 longer variants GenVault is named). PASS.
- **No health claims:** none made (no ancestry/medical-insight claims). PASS.
- **No fear-mongering childfree or parents:** M3 is affirmative ("your story, not your obligation"); M2 mortality-salience is heirloom-warm, not doom. PASS.
- **Flag for legal review before spend:** (1) using "Guinness World Records" by name in paid creative (nominative use of a third-party mark; likely fine as factual news reference, confirm); (2) "stored for decades" duration claim — confirm the GenVault contract term supports it in writing; (3) "first 1,000 members" scarcity claim must match live counter reality when spend turns on.
