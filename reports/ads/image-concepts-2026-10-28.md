**Headline (36 characters, MEASURED):** Most of what you own is replaceable.

**Body (268 characters, MEASURED):** Deeds, passwords, policies, photographs: all of it can be reissued, reset or reprinted. A biological sample of a person who existed cannot. **Support.** The argument is a replaceability audit, not a scarcity pitch. Everything in the first sentence genuinely can be reissued by some institution. The claim in the second sentence is narrow and checkable: a biological sample is not reissuable, because the person it came from is the only source. **No claim is made about what the sample can later be used for.**

**Placement.** Paid rotation approved: no identifiable person, no legible text, no third-party mark anywhere in either frame.

**Ratios.** Square 1080x1080 and a vertical 1080x1920 re-layout. **Both rendered correctly on the FIRST attempt, which has not happened in this lane for four consecutive runs.** The vertical's tighter scale is an improvement rather than a compromise: individual fasteners read larger while the "many interchangeable things" argument survives intact.
