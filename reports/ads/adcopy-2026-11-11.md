## THE WEEK'S COMMERCIAL ANGLE, AND ITS NEAREST NEIGHBOURS

**The angle is the CONDITION attached to a price guarantee.** Not what a thing costs. Not how many prices it has. **What you must do to stop the number moving, and the fact that there is always something.**

**THREE NEIGHBOURS IN THIS LOG WERE OPENED AND NAMED RATHER THAN COUNTED:**
- **30 September, "How Many Prices Does It Have?"**: the axis is the COUNT of prices displayed. **This is not that.**
- **28 October, "The Price Was Set Between Companies"**: the axis is WHO the price was for. **This is not that.**
- **4 November, "Ask It, Then Check It"**: the axis is surface consistency. **This is not that.**

**The new object is a promise about a FUTURE price**, which is neither the price, nor the structure, nor the consistency of the pages that display it.

**SAME-DAY CASCADE, DISCLOSED.** This afternoon's Deep Production pack built an episode on the same finding. **That episode is editorial and asks what the guarantee is worth; this batch is commercial and asks what it costs the buyer to qualify.** **Disclosed here so a later run does not read the overlap as an accident.**

**WHY IT SELLS.** It converts "first 1,000 members lock the founding rate" from a promotional line into **a stated condition of a guarantee**, which is both more honest and more urgent. **It does not require fear, a deadline we invented, or a countdown.**

## (a) META: three concepts, one per audience

### Concept A, audience 25-40, future optionality

- **Primary text, lead (89 characters, measured):** Every price has two numbers. The one you pay, and the one you will be asked to pay later.
- **Longer variant:** Every price has two numbers. The one you pay, and the one you will be asked to pay later. Most sellers are quiet about the second, because nobody can see twenty years out. MySpawn stores a DNA record for $99 a year, billed annually, and the first 1,000 members lock that founding rate. The condition is being early. That is the whole of it.
- **Headline (28):** The Number That Stops Moving
- **Description (23):** $99/year, founding rate
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Visual direction:** a wall thermostat set to one number, hand nowhere near it, room light flat and cool. **`thermostat` returns ZERO in `image-concepts-log.md`.**

### Concept B, audience 40-60, family history and mortality salience

- **Primary text, lead (89 characters, measured):** Ask what it costs to stop the price moving. The answer is never money. It is a condition.
- **Longer variant:** Ask what it costs to stop the price moving. The answer is never money. It is a condition, and conditions do not fit on a pricing page. Ours is stated plainly: the first 1,000 members lock the founding rate of $99 a year, billed annually. Stored by GenVault, an FDA-registered biorepository in New Jersey, at ambient temperature.
- **Headline (29):** What Does The Guarantee Cost?
- **Description (21):** DNA storage, $99/year
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Visual direction:** a single old-fashioned card price tag on a string, lying on a plain dark surface, the written side turned away so no figure is legible. **`price tag` returns ZERO in `image-concepts-log.md`.**

### Concept C, audience childfree legacy

- **Primary text, lead (88 characters, measured):** No kids is not no plan. A record of you, kept under one condition you can actually meet.
- **Longer variant:** No kids is not no plan. A record of you, kept under one condition you can actually meet: be early. MySpawn stores a DNA record for $99 a year, billed annually, and the first 1,000 members lock that founding rate. We store. We do not make promises about what the record becomes, because nobody honestly can.
- **Headline (30):** A Plan That Is Not About Heirs
- **Description (23):** Storage only. $99/year.
- **CTA button:** Learn More
- **Visual direction:** one empty chair in an otherwise full row of identical chairs, shot low and from behind at seat height so **no face and no identifiable person is in frame.** **`empty seat` returns ZERO in `image-concepts-log.md`.**
