## NEW CONCEPTS

### Ad_WhoIsAllowedToBuyThem_145
**Freshness check:** New concept and new headline; acquisition-as-custody-risk has never appeared in an ad. **Visual-hook note, stated rather than glossed:** Ad_CustodyNotAccess_137 (18 September) used a locked door. This is a **detached brass lever on marble**, a fitting rather than a barrier, shot minimal and bright-turned-dark. **Adjacent family, genuinely different image and different idea**; logged so a future run does not make it a third.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** A company in this category filed Chapter 11 in May 2024, emerged because an investor bought the assets, and is operating and expanding in 2026.

- **Headline:** WHO IS ALLOWED TO BUY THEM?
- **Support:** Search any service's terms for the word assign. A closure is visible. A sale is not. Ask us too.
- **Placement:** warm audiences and retargeting. **The final three words invite a question we can only half answer**, which is deliberate.
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** a brushed brass lever handle on marble. **Jeff Steel (@jeffsteel)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-gold-handle-on-a-marble-counter-top-ZqdRa6vjX4Y
- **Why the photo earns it:** a handle is fitted by whoever owns the building, and it stays when they leave. **Tint raised to .64**, far above standard, because the source is white marble in bright light; verified at full size that it now reads as a dark object rather than a catalogue shot.
- **CONDITION, carried from the script package and repeated here:** **if the sample-continuity page is not going to be written, this ad must not run.** The body copy asks the reader to hold us to an answer we have not published.

### Ad_SameAgeLowerNumber_146
**Freshness check:** New concept, new headline, new visual hook. No drink, glass, table-setting or interior-restaurant photo exists in the log. Men's-health statistics have never been an ad subject.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** "Testosterone is dropping one percent a year" is circulating again, and two different one-percent findings are being collapsed into one.

- **Headline:** SAME AGE. LOWER NUMBER. LATER DECADE.
- **Support:** Two one-percent findings get quoted as one. One is a man aging. One is a generation measured lower. Ask which you are being sold.
- **Placement:** cold interest audiences in men's health and longevity. **ZERO product in the body**; the lockup is the only mention. **Not a conversion ad.**
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** a glass of water and a carafe on dark wood, warm bokeh behind. **Clint McKoy (@clintmckoy)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/clear-drinking-glass-on-table-727xRkA3t1s
- **Why the photo earns it:** a glass is a **level**, which is what the headline is about, and the liquid line is the only hard edge in a soft frame.
- **CLAIMS NOTE:** the underlying source is **Travison et al., *Journal of Clinical Endocrinology and Metabolism*, 2007**, *"A Population-Level Decline in Serum Testosterone Levels in American Men"*, Massachusetts Male Aging Study, waves **1987-89, 1995-97, 2002-04**, **age-matched decline of roughly 1.2% per year**, persisting after controls for chronic illness, general health, medications, smoking, BMI, employment and marital status. **Peer-reviewed.** **The ad names no study, no person and no figure**, so it asserts nothing a viewer cannot check, and **it makes no claim about MySpawn and health.** **Verify before running.**
