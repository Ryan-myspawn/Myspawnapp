**Two consequences, and the second one matters more than the first.**

1. **The photo still earns the concept, and arguably earns it better than the description did.** Thirty-six numbered rows with nothing entered on any of them is a stronger picture of "fields exist, substance does not" than an index card holder would have been.

The actual risk was PRINTED SIGNAGE, and it was there, and it is the thing that had to be cropped out.** **The check was right to exist and wrong about what it would find, which is the normal case and is why the check is performed rather than reasoned about.**

This is the fifth time one has been wrong (25 Sep, 30 Sep, 5 Oct correction, and twice since).** The reserve file already says so; **today is the first time the wrongness changed which risk mattered rather than only which words were accurate.**

## VOLUME, HONESTLY, AND TODAY IT CARRIES A NEW FINDING

**One concept and two creatives against a target of ten. Four short days in five: zero on 3 October, two on 4 October, two on 5 October, two today.**

**The lane was LIVE at rung (a) all day, so search is again not the cause.** **One photo earned its concept and one did not, and the one that did not failed on our own anti-repetition rule rather than on quality.**

**THE NEW FINDING, AND IT IS THE REASON THIS RUN CANNOT REACH TEN EVEN IN PRINCIPLE.**

**The brief's own escape hatch for a thin day is "still delivering 10 total via more verticals of the backlog's proven ads." That escape hatch does not exist, and it was checked rather than assumed.**

- **There are backlog squares with no vertical adaptation: roughly twenty of them, from 27 August to 14 September.**
- **Not one of them can be re-rendered.** Every HTML master in that range references its photo as a LOCAL filename (`src="src_books.jpg"` and the like). **The scratchpad directories holding those files are gone, and the master does not record the remote Unsplash URL.**
- **Masters became self-contained on 15 SEPTEMBER.** Every ad from that date carries `src="https://images.unsplash.com/..."` and is re-renderable from the repository alone.
- **And every square from 15 September onward already has its vertical.**

**So the backlog is empty on both sides: the older half is unrecoverable and the newer half is already adapted.** **The volume target's own fallback has been unavailable for three weeks and no run has said so.**

**TWO THINGS FOLLOW.**
1. **The fallback should be rewritten or retired in the brief**, because instructing a run to do an impossible thing produces either a quiet miss or a fabricated number.
2. **The 15 September change is worth naming as the fix that worked.** Recording the remote URL in the master is what makes an ad re-renderable a month later, and it was adopted without being announced. **Everything shipped since is recoverable. Nothing before it is.**

**THE STRUCTURAL ASK IS UNCHANGED AND IS NOW AT ITS TWENTIETH ASKING: the sourcing-only run.** A day spent banking against FAMILIES rather than against one commissioned headline. **Today's reserve yield, three photos of which one is usable, is the same problem in miniature: searching on the day, against one brief, returns a cluster and not a spread.**
