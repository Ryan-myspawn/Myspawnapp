## NEW CONCEPT 1: Ad_TheCautiousVersionCameFirst_182

**Headline:** The cautious version came first. It just travels slower.

**Body:** A June 2026 bioethics paper argues the public conversation about lab-grown eggs runs ahead of the science. One of its co-authors runs the lab that did the mouse work. No lab has produced a mature human egg or sperm from a stem cell. We are not in that business. We store DNA, $99 a year, billed annually.

**Support:** *Changing Discourse on In Vitro Gametogenesis: Expectation, Scientific Reality, and the Ethics of Hype*, **Monash Bioethics Review**, published online **June 2026**, DOI 10.1007/s40592-026-00295-0, PubMed 42247096. Lead author Daisuke Yamashita; **Katsuhiko Hayashi is a CO-AUTHOR, which is the correction this afternoon's Content Factory run made to our own morning radar.** Abstract and excerpts read; full text not opened, so the body paraphrases and claims nothing verbatim.

**Visual hook:** a mess of telephone and power lines crossing a blown-out sky, shot from directly below, inverted to near-black. **The image is the hook: many signals on the same wires, arriving at different speeds.** The headline is the turn.

**Photo:** Unsplash `wkBjPeqe_KQ`, **Jason Hawke (@jfhawke)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-telephone-pole-with-many-wires-wkBjPeqe_KQ, 3349x3349, raw `https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1668990900603-52a164c13b30`. **No people, no faces, no legible brand mark.**

**FILTER INVERSION APPLIED.** The source is a bright white sky, base `#f3f3f3`. The house chain opens dark sources and would have blown this out. Used instead: `brightness(0.44) contrast(1.18) saturate(0)`. **Third consecutive use of the inversion, after Ad_180 and Ad_181.**

**Placement:** paid, all placements. Square 1080x1080; vertical 1080x1920.

## NEW CONCEPT 3: Ad_NobodySetTheInterval_184

**Headline:** Nobody set the interval. So each lab sets its own.

**Body:** There are no guidelines for when a laboratory should re-examine a genetic result it already reported. Laboratories choose their own intervals, and two people can wait very different lengths of time for the same answer. Ask yours what theirs is. **Support:** today's Deep Production explainer, *How a Maybe Becomes a Yes*, which documents the absence of guidelines for the reinterpretation of prior genetic test results and the resulting laboratory-set intervals. **This is the sentence the Deep Production run named as the day's highest-leverage line, and this is the first asset to carry it into paid.**
