## A PRODUCTION TECHNIQUE ADDED TODAY

**Problem:** the forge photograph is a high-grain, high-ISO source. At full resolution it would not compress under the mirror-safe 950KB budget: **q92 produced 2,531KB and it took q60 to fit, which bands badly on a dark image.**

**The wrong fix, tested and rejected:** blurring the exported composite. **It softens the type as well as the photo**, and at blur 1.2 the headline was visibly degraded.

**The fix adopted: a CSS `blur(0.7px)` applied to the PHOTO LAYER ONLY**, inside the existing filter chain, before render. **The type is drawn after the photo and stays sharp.** Result: **the vertical fits at q88 and 811KB, the square at q92 and 481KB.**

**RULE ADDED: when a source is grainy enough to blow the JPEG budget, denoise the photo layer in CSS rather than the exported file.** This is the first time the mirror-safe limit has forced a visual compromise, and it did not have to.
