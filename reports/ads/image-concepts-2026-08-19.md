# Image Ad Concepts — 2026-08-19 (Run #1)

Image Ad Concepts based on: today's radar and ad-lab posts: the 31-year frozen embryo proof story, the heirloom-drawer object lesson, the access-gap brand thesis, the back-to-school Memory Vault seal, and the free Letter to 2126 tool.

**Concept 1 - The 1994 Proof**
Ad code: Ad_Proof_1994_01
Headline: Frozen in 1994. Born in 2025.
Supporting line: Properly stored biology waits. Yours can too. $99.
Visual Description: Split-frame composition. Left half: grainy 1994-VHS-style still of a cryostorage dewar in a dim lab, timestamp corner text "APR 1994", desaturated teal cast, film grain. Right half: warm sunlit close-up of a newborn's hand gripping a parent's finger, soft morning light, shallow depth of field. The halves are divided by a thin vertical indigo-to-blue gradient line (#3B82F6 to #6366F1) with a subtle glow. MySpawn orb logo top left. Headline set in bold white geometric sans across the seam.
Aspect ratios: 1:1 primary, 4:5 for feed, 9:16 story crop with the split running horizontally instead.
Text placement: headline center-seam, supporting line bottom center, logo top left.
Mood notes: documentary meets tenderness; proof, not sci-fi. High contrast, no purple overload; let the gradient divider carry the brand.

**Concept 2 - The Drawer**
Ad code: Ad_Drawer_02
Headline: Everything in this drawer outlives you.
Supporting line: Add the one document your family is written in.
Visual Description: Top-down premium flat-lay of an open wooden desk drawer on dark charcoal background (#0F172A vignette). Inside: a worn passport, a folded birth certificate, three faded family photographs, a brass key, and the MySpawn kit box placed slightly apart with a soft indigo rim-light (#6366F1). Museum-grade lighting, dust motes in a single light shaft. Photoreal, 85mm look. Logo top left in white.
Aspect ratios: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 (drawer shot vertically with items stacked toward bottom).
Text placement: headline top third over the dark wood, supporting line directly beneath in lighter weight, price chip "$99" bottom right.
Mood notes: quiet, heirloom, estate-planning gravity with zero fear; the kit belongs among documents, not gadgets.

**Concept 3 - The Access Gap**
Ad code: Ad_Compare_AccessGap_03
Headline: Billionaires bank their biology. Now you can.
Supporting line: Same accredited storage standard. Seven minutes. $99.
Visual Description: Classic MySpawn "Them vs You" comparison layout on ink-dark background (#0F172A). Left column labeled THEM: silhouette of a private jet stair and an anonymous suited figure, rendered in cold desaturated blue, caption lines "Private biobanks. Family offices. Seven figures." Right column labeled YOU: crisp photoreal MySpawn kit box on a kitchen table in warm light, caption lines "At-home kit. Accredited US biorepository. $99." Thin gradient divider (#3B82F6 to #6366F1). No real or recognizable person anywhere; silhouettes only. Logo top left.
Aspect ratios: 1:1 primary, 4:5; 9:16 stacks THEM above YOU.
Text placement: headline top center spanning both columns, column captions inside each half, supporting line bottom center.
Mood notes: slightly provocative, level-headed, zero envy; the joke is how small the right column's price is. Legal note honored: no named individuals in paid creative.

**Concept 4 - First Day, Sealed**
Ad code: Ad_Vault_FirstDay_04
Headline: She won't remember today. The vault will.
Supporting line: Seal a message now. It opens at graduation.
Visual Description: A single instant-photo print (white border) pinned center-frame: a child with a backpack at a front door, shot from behind, morning light. Around the photo, dark ink background with a soft purple ambient glow. Below the photo, a minimal UI element: a rounded lock chip reading "SEALED UNTIL JUN 2038" in mono type with a small padlock icon, indigo gradient border. Subtle paper shadow. Logo top left. Photoreal photo, flat clean UI overlay.
Aspect ratios: 4:5 primary (parents on feed), 1:1, 9:16 with photo higher and lock chip mid-frame.
Text placement: headline above the photo, supporting line under the lock chip, "myspawn.me/vault" bottom center small.
Mood notes: warm, seasonal (back-to-school week), a lump in the throat but optimistic; the lock chip is the product demo in one glance.

**Concept 5 - The Letter**
Ad code: Ad_Letter_2126_05
Headline: Write a letter that opens in 2126.
Supporting line: Free. No account. Sealed one hundred years.
Visual Description: Near-black canvas (#0B0F1A) with a single line of typewriter-style text mid-frame, cursor still blinking: "To whoever finds this," rendered as glowing white text with a faint indigo halo. Below it, a thin horizontal gradient rule (#3B82F6 to #6366F1) and a small wax-seal icon rendered in the same indigo, embossed with "2126". Vast negative space. Logo top left. Minimalist, typographic, no photography.
Aspect ratios: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16 (the line sits at upper third, seal at lower third).
Text placement: headline bottom third, supporting line beneath it, URL "myspawn.me/letter" smallest, bottom center.
Mood notes: the quietest ad in the set; intrigue over pitch. Built for the childfree-legacy and writer audiences; also the creative for the free-tool lead-gen funnel.
