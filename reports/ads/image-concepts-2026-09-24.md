The vertical uses object-position 30% plus the same brightness, since the canvas is not square there.

### NEW FAILURE MODE, and it is the fourth description rule this month

**`8JM30uYVOp4` is described as "black and silver scissors on brown wooden table". That description is accurate and it omits the entire foreground.** The photograph is taken **through a shop window**: the frame is dominated by a street reflection containing a **legible commercial sign** and vehicles. The tools are real and are behind glass.

**Rule added: an alt description names the SUBJECT and is not a description of the FRAME.** A correct subject line can sit on a photograph whose composition is something else entirely. **Only viewing the image at size catches it.** This joins 21 Sep (read the full description, not the thumbnail), 22 Sep (read for provenance), 23 Sep (read for medium) and today's Trend Radar rule about query-shaped headings.

**Consequence, stated plainly: Ad_ALotteryTicketIsNotAPension_153 is NOT SHIPPED and is HELD, not cancelled.** Its concept is sound and its copy is written in today's Content Factory file. **It needs a hairbrush photograph that is neither a brand's product shoot nor a render, and the first eight candidates were one or the other.** **Next attempt should try the fallback subjects the Content Factory already named: a single hair strand on a tile, or an open bathroom drawer from directly above.**
