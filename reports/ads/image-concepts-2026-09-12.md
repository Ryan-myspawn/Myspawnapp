## Why 9 and not 10

Hooks were NOT thin today — the day produced five strong new concepts, the most in a week. The
shortfall is geometric and legal, and it is worth stating precisely rather than rounding up:

1. **Ad_TheBoringOne_125 cannot be adapted to 9:16.** Its clock spans about 57% of the source frame's
   width. To cover 1920px of height from a 6000x4000 landscape photo the layer must be at least
   2880px wide, which leaves only 1080/2880 = 37.5% of the photo visible horizontally. The clock
   physically does not fit, and a half-cut digital clock reads as a production error, not a crop.
   I tried two repositions before measuring it and concluding it is impossible rather than mistuned.
2. **Ad_PreservedSecret_123 cannot be adapted to 9:16** because its usable region is only the top 42%
   of the frame (see Kill Log), and filling a tall canvas from that band needs roughly a 2x upscale.
3. **The only remaining backlog square without a vertical is Ad_AisleSeven_102**, and its photo
   exposes legible retail category signage throughout plus two people in the aisle. The Sep 8 square
   passed only because of a tight crop; a *tall* frame looks down the aisle and necessarily includes
   both. Verified by inspection today, not assumed.

Three separate hard constraints, none of which I am willing to spend a rail to get around. Nine
finished creatives that all pass is the right outcome.
