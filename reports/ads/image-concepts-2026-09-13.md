## RECOMMENDATION — the volume target needs a decision

At 113 square concepts, six-a-day of genuinely non-repeating new concepts is no longer reliably
achievable against an absolute no-repeat rule, and the failure mode is either padding or a short
batch. Today it was a short batch. Three options, for a founder call:

1. **Keep 10/day and narrow the no-repeat rule** to headlines plus a 30-day visual-hook window,
   allowing deliberate re-executions of proven arguments on new photography.
2. **Lower the daily target** to 4-6 finished creatives and keep the absolute rule.
3. **Add a systematic refresh lane:** re-shoot the best 20 historical concepts on new photography as
   explicitly-logged v2s, which is honest re-execution rather than repetition.

My recommendation is **(3) plus (2)** — the back catalogue is the asset, and re-executing a proven
argument on a better photo is worth more than a 114th novel concept.
