## Ad_NobodyIsNotHoldingIt_162

**Freshness check:** headline, concept and visual hook all new. **"knot" returns zero in the log.** Disclosed adjacency: "rope" returns four hits, but those are a **velvet stanchion rope** (Ad_EligibilityWall_76, 3 September) and Ad_PropertyOnOctoberFirst_130. **A thick mooring knot is a different object in a different register**, and the distinction is stated rather than assumed.

**THIS IS THE FIRST CREATIVE IN THE BANK THAT STATES OUR OWN MODEL'S DOWNSIDE IN ITS OWN BODY COPY.** "Pay yearly and the risk is you" is the sentence a competitor would use against us, printed in our own ad, first in the list. **It is also why the ad is credible**, and it is why the 50-year-old estate planner passes it outright.

## Ad_StillInCommittee_163

**Freshness check:** headline, concept and visual hook all new. **"canal", "lock gate", "turnstile" and "scaffold" all return zero in the log.** No prior ad has used a legislative hook.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55.
**Trend-hook used:** the **Don't Sell My DNA Act, S. 1916**, introduced and referred to the Senate Judiciary Committee on **22 May 2025** and still in committee sixteen months later.

- **Headline:** Introduced May 2025. Still in committee.
- **Support:** The bill to protect genetic data in bankruptcy has not moved in sixteen months. Read your provider's assignment clause instead.
- **Placement:** paid, square and vertical.
- **Photo:** Unsplash `3I0kJCVI6KY`, **H (@hozzapot)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/wooden-canal-lock-with-water-reflection-3I0kJCVI6KY. Sourced fresh today.

**WHY A LOCK GATE EARNS THIS CONCEPT.** A canal lock holds water at a level until somebody operates it. **Nothing is broken and nothing is moving**, which is the exact condition of a bill referred to committee and left there. **The image is the hook; the date is the turn.**
**CLAIMS DISCIPLINE:** the bill number, sponsors, introduction date and referral are **official congressional records**. **The ad makes no prediction about whether it will pass**, and it does not criticize any legislator. The call to action is the thing a reader can do without Congress: read the assignment clause.
