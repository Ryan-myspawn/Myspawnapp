## Compliance pass
- **Claims check:** all pricing figures match the sourced ranges in `reports/blog/sperm-freezing-cost.md` (published same week); no invented numbers.
- **Storage-only boundary:** every unit states storage/record-keeping only; M3 explicitly disclaims any fertility-service connection, which also functions as a compliance safeguard against implying we offer fertility services.
- **GenVault attribution:** "accredited US biorepository" used generically in ad copy (not GenVault by name) per the channel's established paid-ad convention — accreditation claims are the biorepository's, not implied as MySpawn's own certifications.
- **No health claims:** copy makes zero claims about sperm health, fertility outcomes, or DNA testing/analysis — purely a pricing/positioning comparison.
- **No fear-mongering:** tone throughout is informational/comparative, not urgency-based; M3 is deliberately reassuring rather than persuasive-pressure for the childfree segment.
- **Legal review flag:** the specific dollar ranges cited ($300-$1,500, $150-$500/yr, etc.) are sourced to third-party clinic pricing pages as of Aug 2026 and could shift — recommend a quarterly refresh check on the SEARCH ad descriptions and M1's primary text if this angle stays in rotation past this quarter.
