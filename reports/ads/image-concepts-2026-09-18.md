# Image Ad Concepts: 2026-09-18

**Photo lane: RUNG (a) LIVE, MCP search working.** Egress self-test returned **200**. Unsplash MCP search returned usable results on the first two queries. **Nothing was pulled from the reserve today, and nothing was reused.**

**Volume: 10 finished creatives = 5 NEW photo-composited squares + 5 vertical adaptations.** The mandate's default mix is 6 new + 4 verticals; the mix was shifted by one because **five photos earned their concepts and a sixth did not.** The detail is in the QC Gate. This is the fourth consecutive run under the 6-new target, and the cause **changed today**: it was not rights failures (Sep 17) or object availability (Sep 16). It was that a sourcing-first method produced five strong pairings and I declined to invent a sixth.

**Method change made inside this run, and it worked.** Instead of writing six headlines and then hunting a photo for each, this run **searched first, inspected the bank at full size, and then wrote headlines against the photos that survived.** Two searches produced six candidates; five shipped. That is a materially better hit rate than the last three runs and it is the inversion recommended on Sep 15, 16 and 17. **It is not a substitute for the banked-reserve proposal, which would let a run start from 30-40 vetted photos instead of six.**

## NEW CONCEPTS

### Ad_CustodyNotAccess_137
**Freshness check:** No concept, headline or visual hook in the log touches platform legacy settings. The photo (a locked commercial door, close on the lock plate) has never been used.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** TikTok shipped a Legacy Account setting in late August 2026, and the person you name cannot open anything.

- **Headline:** CUSTODY IS NOT ACCESS.
- **Support:** Every platform now lets you name someone for after you are gone. Read what they may actually do: mind the account, open nothing in it.
- **Placement:** Meta and Instagram feed; the strongest cold-audience ad in the batch because the claim is somebody else's fine print, not ours.
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** "Locked Door", a close view of a metal and glass door with the lock plate visible. **Benoit Roy (@beroy)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-close-up-of-a-metal-door-with-a-handle-poPQBXkp0GI
- **Why the photo earns it:** the lock is the subject and the door is unremarkable. It reads as a thing that is shut rather than a thing that is guarded, which is the exact register of the claim.

### Ad_AKeyIsNotAMessage_138
**Freshness check:** New concept, new headline, new visual hook. No keys, padlock or hardware photo appears anywhere in the log.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** Same platform peg, opposite half of the argument: naming a custodian is administration, not inheritance.

- **Headline:** A KEY IS NOT A MESSAGE.
- **Support:** Handing over access is administration. Leaving something behind has to be done on purpose.
- **Placement:** retargeting and warm audiences; it assumes the viewer already accepts the first claim.
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** keys and a padlock lying on weathered wooden planks, near monochrome. **Mihail Dimitrov (@mdimitrov)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-bunch-of-keys-that-are-on-top-of-a-wooden-table-cq8AnWHAKA8
- **Why the photo earns it:** keys are the literal object the headline denies the meaning of. The wood grain gives it age without nostalgia.

### Ad_ItWentIntoOneEye_139
**Freshness check:** ER-100 has never appeared in an ad. No medical-instrument photo exists in the log.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** On 9 June 2026 the first partial reprogramming therapy given to a human went into an eye.

- **Headline:** IN JUNE, IT WENT INTO ONE EYE.
- **Support:** The first partial reprogramming therapy given to a human. Phase 1, safety only, nothing published. We store records only.
- **Placement:** zero-product credibility ad. Interest-based cold audiences in longevity and biotech. **Not a conversion ad and should not be judged on cost per acquisition.**
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** a phoropter, the dial instrument used in eye examinations. **João Melo (@joaosoaresmelo)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-close-up-of-a-pair-of-medical-equipment--OpWKFHA_yQ
- **Why the photo earns it:** the eye is the whole surprise in the fact, and a phoropter is instantly readable as eye medicine without being clinical or frightening.
- **CROP NOTE, and it is a rights fix:** the source frame carries a legible brand mark, **CHAROPS**, on the upper body of the instrument. Rather than kill the photo, the composition uses a **CSS crop of the remote image** (square: 1543px square offset to -154, -463; vertical: 2743px square offset to -831, -823) that excludes the top 30% of the frame where the mark sits. **Verified at full resolution in both ratios: the brand mark is absent, and the only remaining text is dial graduation numbers and a spirit level with no lettering.** Precedent note: TDK, RED and CORBIN were killed outright because the mark was on or beside the focal element. Here the mark is peripheral and croppable, so a crop is the proportionate response rather than a loosening of the rule.

### Ad_NotWeakResultsNone_140
**Freshness check:** New concept, new headline. No corridor or shutter photo in the log.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** The unattributed claim that epigenetic reprogramming therapies are two to five years from approval, against a Phase 1 that began in June with nothing published.

- **Headline:** NOT WEAK RESULTS. NONE.
- **Support:** Told a therapy is two to five years from approval? Ask what has started, what phase it is in, and what has been published.
- **Placement:** the same cold biotech and longevity audiences as 139, as its second beat. Also the best organic post in the batch.
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** a dark corridor lined with closed roller shutters, receding to a lit door. **All Yawn (@allyawn_)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/a-dark-eerie-hallway-with-closed-shop-fronts-BP4_Z0dYmoc
- **Why the photo earns it:** a corridor of things that are all shut is what "none" looks like. **Tint deliberately reduced to .30** because the source is already very dark.

### Ad_TheDateIsTheProduct_141
**Freshness check:** The headline was **banked on 16 September** in `reports/ads/adcopy-2026-09-16.md` as the following week's lead and has never been produced. New visual hook.
**Primary audience:** Men 25-55
**Trend / hook used:** Today's competitor dive established that scheduled delivery on a chosen date is not unique to us. The horizon is.

- **Headline:** THE DATE IS THE PRODUCT.
- **Support:** Anyone can keep a file. The part worth paying for is that it opens on a day you chose, for a person you named.
- **Placement:** Memory Vault prospecting, and the one creative here that sells the product rather than a distinction.
- **Ratios:** 1080x1080 + 1080x1920.
- **Photo:** a dark interior hallway opening onto a bright doorway and daylight. **Md Mahdi (@mahdi17)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/dark-hallway-with-open-doors-leading-to-sunny-outdoors-ARAsw7zFx6w
- **Why the photo earns it:** a lit door at the end of a dark passage is a date you are walking toward. **Tint reduced to .24**; the source is already very dark and the doorway is the only light in the frame, so it survives the gradient.

## RENDER NOTE FOR THE NEXT RUN (save it the hour this cost)
**playwright-core is no longer installed** and `npm i` in the scratchpad silently produced nothing, so the documented pipeline could not run. The working substitute is the Chromium CLI directly:

```
/opt/pw-browsers/chromium-1194/chrome-linux/chrome --headless --no-sandbox --disable-gpu \
  --hide-scrollbars --force-device-scale-factor=2 --allow-file-access-from-files \
  --virtual-time-budget=15000 --window-size=1080,<H+200> \
  --screenshot=out.png file://<abs path>
```
**Two things that will bite anyone who uses it without reading this:** it cannot reach images.unsplash.com, so render against local copies and keep remote URLs in the committed master; and it clips about 87 CSS pixels off the bottom, so render taller and crop to exact size with PIL. **Both failures are silent.**
