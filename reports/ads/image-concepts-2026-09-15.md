## NEW CONCEPT 1: Ad_SeventeenYearsOld_128

**Freshness check:** New. No MySpawn ad has been built on a peaking short-form audio trend, and the "why does this old file still exist" turn has never been used. Visual hook (scattered prints and film strips on a floor) is new to the log.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55.**
**Trend-hook used:** "My American Girl Doll" is the top trending TikTok audio this week, crossing to Instagram Reels; the source clip is a **2009 home video**.

- **Headline:** THE MOST-USED SOUND THIS WEEK IS FROM 2009.
- **Support:** Somebody moved that file every time they changed phones. That is the whole job.
- **Vertical headline:** THIS WEEK'S TOP SOUND IS FROM 2009. / Somebody kept moving that file for seventeen years.
- **Placement:** feed and Stories. Strongest paid candidate in the batch and the only one with an expiry date, because a trending audio is a two-week window.
- **Photo:** scattered photographic prints and 35mm film strips on a wood floor between two boots. **Mathias Reding (@matreding)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/photos-between-pair-of-brown-leather-boots-on-floor-KfVkkjJ2P3c
- **Right-of-publicity note:** the ad references the phenomenon of the trend only. It does not use the original audio, does not show the clip, and does not name or depict the person in it, who was a child in 2009 and is a private adult now.

## NEW CONCEPT 2: Ad_SixHundredAndOneDays_129

**Freshness check:** New. The log has no publication-lag concept and no paper-stack visual.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55**, optimizer lead.

## NEW CONCEPT 3: Ad_PropertyOnOctoberFirst_130

**Freshness check:** New. No MySpawn ad has ever cited a statute or an effective date. Library visual is new to the log.
**Primary audience: Men 25-55**, estate-planner lead.
**Trend-hook used:** Connecticut's genetic-privacy provisions take effect **October 1, 2026**, sixteen days from today.

- **Headline:** OCTOBER 1: YOUR DNA SAMPLE BECOMES PROPERTY.
- **Support:** That is Connecticut's new law. What you can make a company do with your sample still depends on your state.
- **Vertical headline:** YOUR SAMPLE BECOMES PROPERTY ON OCTOBER 1. / Connecticut's new law. Your rights still depend on your state.
- **Placement:** feed and Stories. Run before October 1; it is worth much less afterwards.
- **Photo:** a deep, warm library of bound volumes. **Clint McKoy (@clintmckoy)**, https://unsplash.com/photos/books-in-brown-bookshelf-App9lCSl2uA
- **Claims note, and I rewrote the line once for this:** an earlier draft said Connecticut was "the first" state to create a property right in a biological sample. **That is not established and several states' statutes already speak to property rights**, so the claim was removed before render. The shipped copy asserts only that this is Connecticut's law and that rights vary by state, both of which are safe.
