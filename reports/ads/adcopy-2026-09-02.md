## COMPLIANCE PASS
- **Storage only:** every asset states storage/record framing; no asset promises descendant creation, revival, or any future-science outcome. M3 explicitly frames what the future does as "the future's business."
- **Different products:** M1/M2/Spark 1/Spark 2 all include a different-product line per the PricedAtBirth framing rule (screening/selection vs record storage). Never claims parity or substitution.
- **Accreditations:** attributed correctly: storage BY GenVault, an independent CAP-accredited, ISO 9001/20387, FDA-registered biorepository. Short copy uses "accredited, FDA-registered biorepository" without claiming MySpawn itself holds the accreditations.
- **Third-party prices:** Nucleus $5,999/$30,000/~$40,000 (PRNewswire, CBS, Interesting Engineering; verified 2026-09-02); Herasight ~$50,000 (CBS/Al Majalla, verified 2026-08-31). Refresh before any actual spend if launch is 30+ days out. No competitor named in any consumer-facing asset (prices only); names live in this doc for sourcing.
- **ASRM line (Spark 1):** "the professional body says the science is unproven" is a fair paraphrase of the Dec 2025 ASRM ethics/practice committee conclusion (unproven, should not be used clinically). Keep the paraphrase exactly this modest; do not escalate to "condemned" or "banned."
- **No fear-mongering:** no asset threatens loss, disease, or regret; the childfree asset affirms the choice; the 40-60 asset sells continuity, not mortality dread.
- **Pricing:** $99/year everywhere, founding-rate line included where natural; zero one-time phrasing.
- **LEGAL REVIEW FLAGS:** (1) quoting real third-party prices in paid creative (M1, Sparks) is factual and sourced but worth one legal read before spend; (2) "FDA-registered" must always modify the biorepository, never imply product approval; (3) Spark 1's printed "price list" prop must be clearly generic (no competitor logos) when filmed.
