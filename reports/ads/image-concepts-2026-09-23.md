## THE VOLUME LINE, AND TODAY IT IS NOT A COMPLAINT, IT IS A TEST RESULT

**Two finished against a target of ten, and the lowest since 17 September.** I am not going to soften it, because **today produced the cleanest evidence yet for the request I have now made eight times, and the evidence is that my own fix failed at its first real test.**

**Yesterday I banked eight photographs into the reserve** and wrote this caveat into the file at the time:

> *"Six of the eight are surfaces. That is the family the day's queries happened to be in, and a reserve of eight surfaces does not solve the repetition problem, which is precisely the argument for a dedicated sourcing run across families rather than incidental banking."*

**Today that prediction came true in full. Not one of the eight reserve entries was usable**, because every single one is a surface or a piece of architecture, and both families are spent: architecture is blocked until **29 September** by Ad 148, and the light-split-on-a-surface family was killed yesterday inside its own batch.

**That is what a reserve built incidentally does. It banks more of whatever you were already looking at.** A reserve that solves the repetition problem has to be built **deliberately, across families, in one pass**, which is exactly what the sourcing-run request has asked for since 15 September.

**Second cause, equally concrete: concept 149 is now held for a second day and I have searched two entirely different visual families for it.** Architecture was killed for repetition yesterday. **Textile and paper, briefed this afternoon by the Content Factory, produced nothing that earns the concept.** The best paper result was a generic pen-on-blank-page, which the photo mandate forbids by name: *"no generic stock-feel pairings."* **The concept is good, the copy is written, and it has no image. It stays held rather than shipping a pairing that does not earn itself.**

**Standing recommendation, ninth asking, wording unchanged:**

> **(1) authorize ONE sourcing-only run banking 30-40 vetted photos, roughly a week of headroom at the observed clearance rate; (2) lower the daily target to 4-6 new concepts plus a refresh lane; (3) keep the kill rules exactly as they are, because every shortfall this month came from enforcing them and every kill was correct.**

**Today adds the strongest argument the request has had: incidental banking demonstrably does not work, and we now know that from evidence rather than from reasoning.**

## SEARCH NOTE 2026-09-23

**Rule added, and it belongs beside the two from this week:** **read the description for MEDIUM as well as for content and provenance.** A render is not a photograph, the mandate is explicit that all creatives are built on real photography, and **a thumbnail cannot tell you the difference.** This joins **21 September** (read the full description, not the visual) and **22 September** (read for provenance: a brand's product shoot carries that brand with no logo in frame).
