## THE FALLBACK FAILED TOO, AND THE REASON IS A PROPERTY OF OUR OWN ARCHIVE

The brief's instruction for a thin day is explicit: **keep quality on new concepts and still deliver ten by shipping more verticals of the backlog's proven ads.** That was attempted and it is not currently possible.

**FOURTEEN proven squares in the backlog have no vertical adaptation**, which looked like a clean fourteen-creative opportunity: Ad_NotTheAverage_82, Ad_NoReissue_85, Ad_BoringBox_91, Ad_MissingLineItem_92, Ad_OffTheTape_96, Ad_FourQuestions_99, Ad_AisleSeven_102, Ad_WeCorrected_106, Ad_TransferOfOwnership_108, Ad_OneIn2700_111, Ad_TwoDishes_114, Ad_ThirtyYears_118, Ad_PreservedSecret_123, Ad_TheBoringOne_125.

**None of them can be re-rendered.**

**THE FINDING, stated plainly: 72 of the 167 committed HTML masters reference a LOCAL source file, such as `src_office.jpg` or `src_vault.jpg`, rather than a remote Unsplash URL.** Those files lived in per-run scratchpad directories. **Scratchpad directories before 12 September no longer exist, and the ones that survive do not hold the specific sources these ads need.**

**The MIRROR-SAFE COMMIT RULE says "the HTML sources are the masters; PNGs are re-renderable."** **For 72 of 167 masters, roughly 43 percent of the archive, that is not true.** They render as a broken image on a navy ground.

**CHECKED AND CONFIRMED, not assumed:** of the **95 masters that reference a remote `images.unsplash.com` URL, EVERY ONE already has a vertical.** The squares missing verticals and the masters that cannot be re-rendered are **the same set**. The only surviving pre-14-September scratchpad source, `src_scaffold.jpg`, belongs to **Ad_EveryBetHasADate_121, which already has its vertical.**

**THE GAP IS ALREADY CLOSED PROSPECTIVELY AND NOBODY NOTICED.** The current photo template writes the remote Unsplash URL into the master, which is why every ad from the remote-URL era is re-renderable. **The damage is historical and cannot be repaired: those source photos are gone from this machine and the masters do not record enough to re-fetch them.**

**PROPOSED, not adopted unilaterally: the 72 affected masters get a one-line header comment recording that they are not re-renderable, so a future run does not plan a batch around them as this one did.** That is a cheap, honest annotation rather than a rewrite.
