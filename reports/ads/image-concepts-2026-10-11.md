**THE BATCH DEFECT, AND IT IS THE SAME ONE AS 9 OCTOBER: all four first renders had the headline running straight through the body copy.** Root cause identical to Ad_ItWorkedAcrossACrowd_186: **the sub is positioned at a fixed `bottom` and the head at another fixed `bottom`, so an over-long sub grows UPWARD into the headline.** **My drafts ran 430 to 500 characters against a template that tolerates roughly 300.** All four were cut by about 40 percent and re-rendered clean.

**THIS IS NOW A TEMPLATE PROBLEM, NOT A WRITING PROBLEM, AND IT SHOULD BE FIXED IN THE TEMPLATE.** Twice in three shipping days the same defect has appeared and been fixed by editing copy. **PROPOSED, for the next run that touches the generator: give the sub a `max-height` with a hard character budget enforced in the Python, so an over-long sub fails at generation time instead of silently colliding at render time.** A defect that is invisible in the code and only appears in a screenshot is exactly the kind the visual-verify step exists for, and exactly the kind that should not need it.
