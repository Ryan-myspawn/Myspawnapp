## THE UNDERLYING CAUSE, AND A CONCRETE FIX

The immediate causes have been different every day: rights and brand failures on Monday, missing objects on Tuesday, both plus a judgment call today. **The underlying cause is the same one and it is structural: we source photographs concept-first, on the day, under a daily deadline.** That guarantees the failure mode we keep hitting, because the specific image a specific headline needs is often the one Unsplash does not have, and the alternative is always either a compromise or a kill.

**The fix is not to try harder on the day. It is to invert the order.**

1. **Run one dedicated sourcing session** that banks **30 to 40 vetted, full-size-inspected, brand-free, face-free photographs** with subject notes, ignoring concepts entirely.
2. **Then write headlines against the bank**, which is how every functioning art department has ever worked.
3. **Keep the daily run for composition and QC**, which are the parts that work reliably.

The reserve exists and is doing exactly this job when it has stock: **the one creative that shipped yesterday's batch and two of the three that shipped Tuesday came from it.** It is simply too small, because it is topped up as a by-product of days that are already failing.

**Recommendation, and this is now the third consecutive run making a version of it, so I am stating it as a decision request rather than an observation: lower the daily target to 4-6 new concepts, add a refresh lane of logged v2s, and authorise one sourcing-only run to build the bank. Without the bank, the target is not achievable honestly, and I would rather say that than keep shipping declining numbers with good explanations attached.**
