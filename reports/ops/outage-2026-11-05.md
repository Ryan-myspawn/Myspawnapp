## What happened

**The session's worker process restarted repeatedly between roughly 15:04 and 23:11 UTC on 5 November.** Each restart arrived mid-turn and the work in progress was lost. **Four scheduled triggers fired in that window and none of them produced a file, a commit or a push.**

**Verified by directory listing rather than asserted: the newest files in each lane are dated 4 November, and `reports/ads/` has no `image-concepts-2026-11-05.md`.**

## What is NOT being done about it, and why

**These four packs are not being written retroactively today.**

**The reason is the record rather than the effort.** Each of those briefs is explicitly dated and explicitly sourced from "the last 24 to 48 hours". **A pack written on 6 November and filed as 5 November would be a dated artefact whose date is false, in a repository whose entire value to this operation is that its dated claims can be checked.** This fleet has spent two weeks refusing unverifiable figures in published copy. **Backdating its own output would be a larger version of the same error.**

**What is lost is genuinely lost: one episode pack, one short-form package, one evening package and one day of ad creatives.** **Five November has six completed lanes on the record (inbox, blog, longposts, newsletter, trend radar, competitor watch) and four missing ones, and that is the honest shape of the day.**

## What the day DID produce, for context

Six lanes completed and pushed before the restarts began: the inbox log, blog article 79, longposts #190 to #194, newsletter issue 12, the trend radar, and the competitor watch. **The three creative lanes that failed are the three that sit latest in the day.**

## The observation worth keeping

**The lanes that failed are the lanes that run longest.** The image-ad run downloads and renders; the production and script runs draft several thousand words each. **A long run has more wall-clock exposure to a restart than a short one, so an unstable worker does not remove output evenly: it removes the most expensive output first.**

**This compounds a finding already on the record from the same morning.** Yesterday's trend radar reported that the research-consuming lanes now run before the radar and leave it nothing. **Today adds the other half: the lanes scheduled latest are also the ones most likely to be cut off.** **Both are scheduling facts, both are founder actions, and neither can be fixed from inside a session.**

**NOTHING IS RECOMMENDED HERE BEYOND VISIBILITY.** Re-running a missed trigger, changing a schedule and pausing a lane are all founder actions. **This file records what did not happen, so that the next person reading the dated folders does not have to guess.**
