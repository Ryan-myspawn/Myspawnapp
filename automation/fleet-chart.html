How the day chains together

Research feeds content: Trend Radar (13:00) supplies concepts to Deep Production (15:00), Content Factory (17:00), Image Ads (18:00) and the PM run (23:00). Ad Copy Lab angles feed the image agent's no-repeat checks.
Memory prevents repetition: every agent checks its log before producing and updates it after; topics, hooks, formats and concepts are never reused.
Humans hold the only publish button: agents write, render, draft and deliver internally; posting, sending and paid spend always pass Jig or Abhinay. Google Analytics closes the measurement loop once the site tag is installed.
